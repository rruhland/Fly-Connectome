What this demonstration does—and doesn’t—show

A single frozen linear policy chooses the left paddle’s motor drive from the existing visual representation. It learned without backpropagation or hidden ball/paddle coordinates.

Interception results apply to the recorded native test cohorts. They do not establish sustained rallying, human-opponent performance, a from-scratch training recipe, or a continuous multi-game learner. Earlier checkpoints used different cohorts; their numbers are not a single continuous learning curve.

Vision shows current camera beliefs. The command readout is the last applied action, from the preceding camera. Forecasts are sensor-derived beliefs, not a privileged ball trajectory.
