The native-score search and both integrity audits completed before registration.

and tag 29118 are fixed prospectively, subject to the full-width cumulative
exclusion check. Expand the formal cohort only during sealed staging, and start
its clock only after the independent preclock metadata review.

The intervention is one constant epsilon change, .2 to .02, in the existing
true-online SARSA learner. Start from the predetermined experienced 20k projected
weights and owned visual memory used by the completed ON/OFF experiment. Install
weights before the initial observation and pending action; reset traces and start
new episodes. Keep visual learning enabled, alpha .001, lambda .995, gamma .95 per
physical second, the existing frozen transform and radial cap 256, ordinary camera
inputs, and native elapsed-time survival reward. Failure is terminal; the 5-second
safety horizon is truncated and bootstrapped. No teacher labels, physics policy
inputs, hit rewards, backpropagation, representation change, or parameter sweep.

Intentionally reuse environment root 1083294751 and learner root 1647809237. This
explicit exception repeats the .2 control's declared training streams. The two
domains remain independent of one another. Changed actions, episode lengths,
exploratory draw branches, and learned visual memories can make later states and
RNG consumption differ; this is one counterfactual training-root comparison, not
inference across independently trained seeds. A positive result would not isolate
on-policy risk from changed state coverage or visual learning.

Reuse the audited .2 ON training endpoint and 5k/10k checkpoints, without retraining
or selecting among them. Pin the original source/runtime, exact inputs, complete
training journal/report/export hashes, own completion verification and independent

and measured wall eligibility are necessary for a valid endpoint. Partial work
and middle exports remain retained and labeled; there is no replacement endpoint,
best-checkpoint selection, seed replacement or extra training from reused cost.

After training, evaluate frozen greedy policies on 64 prospectively pinned fresh B
initial states shared by the .02 endpoint, reused .2 endpoint, common E20k start
and five unchanged simple controls: negative, no-op, positive, random1 and random10.
This is 512 new B trials. Evaluate both conditions' 5k/10k checkpoints on the fixed
first 32 states, another 128 trials. These curves are descriptive and evaluated
only after the dose; they cannot stop or select training. Each checkpoint owns its
actual learned visual snapshot; frozen evaluation never updates it. Preserve
explicit state/index ordering before paired statistics.

For each endpoint keep the original competence screen: mean survival at least 3 s,
at least 50% reaching the 5-second limit, mean lift at least 1 s over the strongest
simple control, and paired 95% CI lower bound above 0. Recompute the strongest
control in each of 10,000 state-cluster bootstrap draws. Report continuous effects
regardless of this gate. Separately estimate endpoint-minus-E20k mean survival and
cap fraction, and .02-minus-.2 mean survival and cap fraction. A clear
lower-exploration development signal requires both latter CI lower bounds above 0;
it does not replace competence or establish population reproducibility.

Run 64 new frozen A probes with the fixed original A head, transform and cap on the
same explicitly reused development serves, without A retraining, using the .02
endpoint memory. Reuse the already verified .2 ON40k A64 results. The common E20k
A reference is retained via pinned annotations, the ON/OFF proof chain and its

to this same reference, report paired first/all-return effects, and make no formal
noninferiority claim. All policy/configuration/content/source identity checks must
pass before reuse. If they fail, hold staging for review rather than silently
reexecute or bypass the identity requirement. Total new evaluation allocation is
704 trials; there are 64 inherited A control trials and 64 inherited E20k A trials.

Fresh DEV/action/bootstrap roots and a full uint32 exclusion snapshot are pinned
only after the native-score result/audits. Exclude all actual prior observations,
roots and calibration/preflight IDs, including current native training/evaluation,
and all reserved final roots. Do not expand final streams. The only reuse exception
is the two declared old training roots. Retained summary/evidence pins make later
zero-new-work invocations preserve the original clocks and eligibility.

Interpret this as a finite genuine reward-learning intervention. A null does not
prove representation impossibility, and neither CI crossing 0 proves equivalence
or retention. Do not follow it with an epsilon grid. A distinct next hypothesis,
if warranted, is reward-only policy optimization of complete matched-start
episode survival; survival normalized by a fixed camera dose is nearly constant
and would be an unsuitable objective. No such allocation is registered here.
