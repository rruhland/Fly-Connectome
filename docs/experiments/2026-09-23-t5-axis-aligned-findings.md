# Fixed local current recovers transferable vertical T5 OFF tuning

The [separate axis-aligned protocol](2026-09-23-t5-axis-aligned-protocol.md) kept the frozen checkpoint, measured graph, current-driven Tm9 graded release, eight-tick Tm4/Tm9 local order, gain 8,000, current cap 1.0, blank calibration, and eight neural ticks/frame unchanged. It matched OFF-bar motion to the functional T5 subtype axes identified by [Maisak et al.](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature12320). The [compact result](2026-09-23-t5-axis-aligned-results.json) records all equal-event opposite trajectories, static-onset controls, per-cell sign counts, gate-current summaries, and blank activity. No long-term weight changed.

**Vertical T5c/d passed both calibration and the withheld position/speed screen.** Up-versus-down aggregate spike contrasts were T5c **-48/-89** and T5d **+58/+49** at y=10/y=22. At the untouched y=16 holdout, contrasts remained **-56/+49** at one pixel/frame and **-25/+12** at two pixels/frame. More individual cells preferred the corresponding direction than the reverse in every case (for example, 31 versus 6 T5c and 21 versus 9 T5d at the faster holdout). Preferred moving-bar counts were 2.85–4.25× static-bar counts. Opposite directions supplied exactly 312 events each at speed one and 624 each at speed two; blank T5 firing was 2.33×10⁻⁶ spikes/neuron/tick. The gate current itself was larger in each subtype's preferred direction.

A frozen gate-off control shows that this is a physical effect of the local interaction rather than pre-existing T5 tuning. At y=10/y=22 and held-out y=16 speeds one/two, gate-off T5c/T5d contrasts were **-1/+7, -8/-6, +3/+3, and +3/+3**, respectively. The gate-on signs and magnitudes arose without direction labels, synthetic edges, backpropagation, or learned weights. The interaction still uses a proposed local dendritic operation and blank-derived threshold, so it is an opt-in mechanistic result rather than a validated biological parameterization.

**Horizontal T5a/b failed.** At x=18 the contrasts were -8/+2; at x=46 they flipped to +15/-3. Moving/static ratios were above 2.9, but the direction contrast was weak and inconsistent, so the protocol stopped before horizontal x=32 holdout. The previous horizontal T5c/d screen also remains failed; reassigning the correct subtype axes did not erase that result.

This is the first frozen, causal T5 spike-level motion feature in the current M1A investigation that transfers to a withheld position and faster motion. It satisfies only the OFF vertical slice of the proposed **feature-expression** gate. Horizontal T5 and T4 ON motion remain unresolved, and no local online prediction learning has been demonstrated. The next bounded test should ask whether the existing local plasticity and next-frame objective actually improve anticipation when this vertical T5 signal is present, using a controlled vertical moving-edge task, its own frozen comparator, and a withheld position/tempo. If not, the measured CT1 inhibitory route omitted by the soma-side roster and the sensory-to-predictive credit path need separate examination before production promotion.
