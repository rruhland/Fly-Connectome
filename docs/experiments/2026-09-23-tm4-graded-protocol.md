# Bounded opt-in Tm4 current release at T5

The [source-state audit](2026-09-23-tm4-subthreshold-findings.md) found local Tm4 current at fast T5d/up motion-gate events that lack a Tm4 spike-history arm. It also found stronger current at static-bar gate events. Test whether a fixed local release law improves actual T5 arm coverage without harming motion specificity. This is an experimental network only; the production graph, signs, delays, spike mode, and plasticity stay unchanged.

Keep the pinned checkpoint, Tm4/Tm9 rest currents 0.85, Tm9 current release cap 0.10, fixed eight-tick T5 order law, and eight neural ticks per camera frame. On **existing feedforward Tm4→T5 edges only**, add a graded supplement after the edge's recorded delay. At source tick `t`, release `clip((I_Tm4(t) − EMA_250ms(I_Tm4)(t−1) − q99_blank)/0.02, 0, cap)` only when that Tm4 neuron does **not** spike at `t`; its spike output remains unchanged. The nonnegative supplement uses each measured edge's existing magnitude and transmitter sign. `q99_blank` comes from that cell's positive current residual on the final 128 blank ticks of a 256-tick Tm9-only settlement. No stimulus or direction label sets the release.

Try caps 0.01, 0.03, and 0.10 from the same restored Tm9-only state. Select the largest whose last-128-tick bright-blank T5c/d added-impulse p99 is at most 0.05, total T5c/d blank spike rate is below 0.01/neuron/tick, and state is finite. Then settle 256 blank ticks at the selected cap and enable the unchanged T5 order gate after its blank calibration. Prepare a comparator identically but without Tm4 graded release. Restore each settled state before every 18-frame matched bright-blank, up/down moving dark-bar, or static dark-bar trial at y=10 and 22 (speed 1), and y=16 (speeds 1 and 2). Use the existing anatomical T5c/d local-cell map. Record total source and target counts, Tm4 arm-history support at the exact baseline future-gate samples, direction contrasts, per-cell majority, moving/static spike ratios, blank spike rates, and finite state. Frozen long-term weights must match the pinned checkpoint.

The experiment passes only if (1) Tm4 arm support on **baseline-defined** future T5d/up gate events at y=16/speed 2 increases by at least eight percentage points and reaches 55%; (2) both T5c/d keep opposite and position/speed-consistent direction-contrast signs with at least ten spikes and per-cell majority agreement in every moving condition; (3) each subtype's preferred-moving/static spike ratio stays at least 1.5 and at least 90% of its matched comparator value in all four conditions; and (4) bright-blank T5 spike rate stays below 0.001/neuron/tick in each condition. This is a frozen motion-feature gate, not evidence of prediction learning. Failure leaves the production architecture unchanged and calls for reassessing the Tm4 source state rather than tuning caps on motion labels.
