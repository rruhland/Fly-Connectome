# Bounded T2 release reaches Li15, but the next spike boundary remains silent

The [preregistered opt-in protocol](2026-09-23-t2-graded-propagation-protocol.md) used the original measured M1 graph and pinned frozen checkpoint. The [results](2026-09-23-t2-graded-propagation-results.json) identify the inputs and all dot/bar/static responses. Every one of the 822 T2 cells could release onto its **27,089 existing outgoing edges** (222,049 contacts), with its recorded excitatory sign, existing delay, contact-scaled magnitude, and pathway-specific current kernel. No connection or trainable parameter was added to the in-place model.

The largest cap meeting the blank-only screen was **0.03**. At that cap, the p99 absolute T2-added Li15 impulse on blank was 0.0242, below the preset 0.05 limit, and Li15 emitted zero blank spikes during the 128-tick calibration window. Cap 0.10 exceeded the impulse limit at 0.0860. All calibrated states were finite.

Restoring the settled core and graded state reproduced a second blank trial exactly, including release, current, voltage, impulses, and spikes. The frozen weights and checkpoint checksum were unchanged.

In matched moving-dot trials, the positive T2 release signal reached Li15 at both x=18 and x=46 for ON and OFF. The p90 per-cell Li15 voltage modulation was 0.00385/0.00857 at x=18 ON/OFF and 0.00454/0.00963 at x=46. Li15's mean signed current also rose by 0.00154/0.00347 and 0.00123/0.00381, respectively. This is meaningful subthreshold propagation beyond the formerly silent T2 spike boundary, without a change to measured signs or topology.

The **preregistered partial T2→Li15 screen failed**. With graded feedback active, local T2 release exceeded its matched-blank p99 in only 4.0% and 3.5% of ON-dot samples at x=18 and x=46, below the 5% source criterion (OFF reached 7.3% and 8.8%). Mean absolute T2-added Li15 impulse modulation was only 0.00014–0.00037, below the 0.002 criterion, though temporal integration made the per-cell voltage response larger. Li15 emitted **zero spikes** in all four dot trials, so its recorded inhibitory output still cannot reach the next stage. MeLo10 remained effectively flat because its main measured afferent class is T2a, whose candidate source signal failed the prior screen. Bars evoked stronger Li15 voltage than dots (p90 0.0167–0.0534); OFF static dots also evoked some response. No object or motion selectivity is claimed.

The narrow next question is whether Li15's *new* local voltage/current state has a blank-separated causal release signal under the graded T2 pilot. Measure that at both locations/polarities, including bar/static controls, before trying inhibitory graded output or LC11. The approved amendment's transmission and feature gates remain unmet, so the production architecture, learning rule, and checkpoint stay unchanged.
