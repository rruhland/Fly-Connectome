# Li15 has a local current signal after T2 transmission, hidden by all-cell pooling

The [all-21-cell protocol](2026-09-23-li15-local-release-protocol.md) and [result](2026-09-23-li15-local-release-results.json) reused the frozen T2 graded pilot at its blank-selected 0.03 cap. No Li15 edge or production state changed. Positive Li15 synaptic current relative to its own causal 250 ms baseline was the strongest candidate. It exceeded each cell's matched-blank p99 in 5.8%, 10.0%, 2.7%, and 7.2% of all-cell samples for x=18 ON/OFF and x=46 ON/OFF, versus 0.7% on blank. The x=46 ON value missed the predeclared 3% screen, so **no all-cell candidate passed**. The other candidate variables also failed.

The [wiring-only follow-up](2026-09-23-li15-afferent-locality-protocol.md) selected Li15 cells from measured T2→Li15 contact mass, using the T2 visual columns inferred from measured upstream anatomy. Nine Li15 cells were dominated by x=18 inputs, nine by x=46, and three were tied. The groups were fixed without response labels. In the [local result](2026-09-23-li15-afferent-locality-results.json), positive current residual passed the **unchanged** four-condition source-information screen: fractions above matched-blank p99 were 12.6%/20.7% at x=18 ON/OFF and 5.4%/16.6% at x=46, versus 0.7% on blank. Mean absolute local dot-minus-blank modulation was 0.00298/0.00498 and 0.00174/0.00601, all above the preset 0.0005 minimum. Positive voltage residual still failed x=46 ON, so the current variable matters.

These results identify a causal, cell-local state that could modulate Li15's **existing inhibitory output** without changing its transmitter sign. They do not show that such output remains quiet on blank, reaches its targets, or improves T4/T5 motion and M1A learning. Moving bars drove much stronger local positive current than dots (0.0127–0.0385 mean absolute modulation); OFF static dots also drove some signal. No small-object selectivity is claimed. The T2→Li15 partial transmission gate still failed, and the production model remains spike-only.

A separately versioned opt-in Li15-output pilot is now justified if the object/surround branch is prioritized. However, the approved amendment sets **T4/T5 motion recovery first**. The immediate milestone investigation should apply this same lesson—preserve local, wiring-defined E/I state rather than globally pool silent cells—to Mi4/Tm4/Tm9 and T4/T5 with frozen impulse and moving-bar controls. No M1A integration is warranted yet.
