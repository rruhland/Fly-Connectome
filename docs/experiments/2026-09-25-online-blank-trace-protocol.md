# Online local event learning from continuously available motion trace

The frozen learned motion trace contains enough pre-reappearance information for an offline 17×17 local decoder to reach 0.516 F1 on unseen three-frame gaps, but the current online readout is raw-onset gated and emits nothing on the last blank frame. A single local amplitude normalization did not transfer jointly across gap lengths. This opt-in experiment asks whether **online, delayed local plasticity** can learn an event forecast directly from the continuously available trace, including when current sensory input is blank.

Keep the saved learned motion dictionary and trace dynamics frozen. Add an experimental zero-initialized, translation-shared 24-channel-to-ON/OFF 17×17 local emission field. Every nonzero trace site contributes through its own learned weights; on the next frame, update those weights using only that site's previous trace activity and the local next-event error. Use one training pass, learning rate 0.5, weight clamp [0,1], and scoring threshold 0.5. Train on 96 generic clean episodes plus 64 interruptions each at two-, three-, and four-frame gaps, covering four training shapes, four directions, two speeds, and two backgrounds. No direction, shape, gap, object, or Pong label enters the update.

Evaluate unseen shapes on 48 center-position cases per gap, the 144-case three-frame multi-position suite, and the unchanged full-scene robustness suite. Report last-blank-frame → reappearance and observed-reappearance → next-event F1, complete-sequence F1, and quiet-target false alarms. Compare the learned trace output with its zero-weight state, a trace-reset copy, archived learned correlation input, fixed raw-event correlation, and pixelwise fixed-plus-trace output. The trace-reset control tests causal history use; the fixed primitive remains a diagnostic, not the learned representation.

An online candidate is viable only if it reaches at least 0.20 gap-exit F1 on the familiar gap, 0.15 on the early gap, and 0.10 on the late gap; adds at least 0.05 F1 to fixed raw on complete three-frame occlusion scenes; keeps all non-occlusion full-scene families within 0.03 F1 of fixed raw; and produces fewer than twice fixed raw's quiet false-alarm pixels. These are gates for further investigation, not production approval. If the one-pass local rule underfits even training gap exits or transfers poorly, do not tune a broad parameter sweep; test a learned recurrent state transition with separate hidden-state and event-emission credit. No in-place M1A architecture changes.
