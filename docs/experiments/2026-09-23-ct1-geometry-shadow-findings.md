# Geometry-routed CT1 still fails the frozen null-suppression gate

The [fixed opt-in geometry-shadow protocol](2026-09-23-ct1-geometry-shadow-protocol.md) used the same local CT1 current/release rule as the earlier column shadow. It reassigned each of **37,186 measured CT1→T5c/d contacts** to the inferred column of its nearest eligible measured Tm1/Tm9 input site on CT1, aggregating into 7,365 compartment-to-T5 entries. Contact totals, GABA-negative output, excitatory source signs, graph gain, two one-tick delays, blank calibration, Tm9 release, and T5 order current were fixed. The off arm reproduced every earlier off-arm spike count, and all blank T5 groups remained silent. A small causal/locality test also passed. The [frozen result](2026-09-23-ct1-geometry-shadow-results.json) did not pass the eight-part rescue screen.

Null-direction spike reductions for T5c were **4.4%, 7.3%, 4.5%, 4.6%** at y=10/y=22 and withheld y=16 speeds one/two. T5d reductions were **3.0%, 2.7%, 2.5%, 1.3%**. Preferred-direction retention was 93.8–97.5%, and direction signs survived, but no condition reached the predeclared 10% null-reduction requirement for both subtypes. Episode-mean CT1 inhibition was null-greater in only three of eight subtype/condition cases, generally by a small amount. The geometry route therefore does **not** rescue frozen T5 motion under the fixed profile, despite the strong anatomical null-side offset.

The result narrows the problem but does not identify the missing mechanism. Two opposite bars traverse the same locations in reverse time, so an episode-mean CT1 current can be nearly equal while its *phase relative to local T5 excitation* differs. The next read-only diagnostic should align CT1 current to each T5 cell's own non-CT1 feedforward arrival, compare pre-event and post-event inhibition in preferred/null trials, and check whether that timing differs across the two locations and holdout speeds. Do not tune CT1 gain, radius, nearest-neighbor count, or release kinetics on this result. No in-place architecture change is supported.
