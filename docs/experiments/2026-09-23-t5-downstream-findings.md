# T5 motion reaches LPi, but the available recurrent credit is inhibitory and intermittent

The [measured-wiring audit](2026-09-23-t5-downstream-wiring-protocol.md) found a clear next-stage route from the newly tuned T5 cells. In the [frozen graph](2026-09-23-t5-downstream-wiring-results.json), 58 LPi34 cells receive **47,281 T5c contacts** and 33 LPi43 cells receive **20,628 T5d contacts**, all from fixed positive T5 transmitter signs. These same LPi populations have respectively 290 and 508 measured incoming predictive edges. LPi3412 receives both T5 subtypes and was retained as a mixed control.

The [fixed dynamic probe](2026-09-23-t5-downstream-probe-protocol.md) confirmed that motion-bearing T5 spikes arrive physically. LPi34 down-minus-up **feedforward** contrasts were **-15.08/-22.84** at y=10/y=22 and **-17.15/-10.07** at withheld y=16 speeds one/two. LPi43 contrasts were **+8.96/+6.75** and **+7.18/+5.01**. The measured T5-spike component accounted for nearly all these contrasts, and the gate-off contrasts were small. All eight preregistered LPi transmission checks passed in the [probe result](2026-09-23-t5-downstream-probe-results.json). LPi3412 mixed both channels and had no stable opposed signal. No LPi34/43 soma spikes occurred in the probes.

The transmission pass is **not** a learning pass. LPi34/43 had no actually arriving predictive edges at y=22; at y=10 or y=16, arrivals were sparse and almost exclusively inhibitory. A one-time [fixed extension](2026-09-23-t5-downstream-extension-protocol.md) to LLPC2, LLPC3, and VS found that all three also inherit gate-dependent T5 direction contrasts ([result](2026-09-23-t5-downstream-extension-results.json)). LLPC2/3 still had absent or highly uneven predictive arrivals across positions and no soma spikes. VS had predictive arrivals in every tested direction/position, but **0–1 excitatory arrivals versus 18–52 inhibitory arrivals** per moving condition; its measured predictive contact mass is 9,014 inhibitory versus 140 excitatory. The other tested downstream pools are likewise predominantly inhibitory by contact mass. These are measured **opponent inputs**, not evidence that a positive T5 feedforward arrival can be forecast by increasing their fixed-sign magnitudes. That last interpretation is an inference from the sign and arrival data, not a physiological proof of their exact computational role.

Another 40-episode `positive feedforward target × inhibitory recurrent eligibility` run at these pools would test a known sign mismatch, not a promising learner. The next architecture investigation should separate **anticipatory excitation** from **null-direction inhibition** at the optic-lobe motion stage, preserving both on measured edges and keeping credit local. It should first audit T5/LPi inhibitory-current timing relative to preferred/null T5 spikes, including the omitted measured CT1 route, then preregister a bounded local objective for each current channel. The current production graph and learning rule stay unchanged. Milestone 1 still lacks horizontal T5/T4 motion and effective online prediction.
