# Bounded Tm9 release exposes a transferable T5 temporal-order signal

The [opt-in protocol](2026-09-23-tm9-graded-protocol.md) replaced Tm9 spike output on its **12,232 existing measured edges** (132,931 contacts) with a nonnegative release fraction from each Tm9 cell's own positive synaptic-current residual. Recorded signs, delays, pathways, and contact magnitudes were retained. The pinned checkpoint, production dynamics, and learning rule were unchanged. The [result](2026-09-23-tm9-graded-results.json) identifies the graph/checkpoint and records calibration, per-cell target responses, repeated-blank state equality, and the fixed order score.

The largest tested cap, **0.10**, passed the blank-only calibration: p99 absolute Tm9-added T5 impulse was 0.012, below the preset 0.05 limit, and blank T5 firing was approximately 0.000005 spikes/neuron/tick. Source release exceeded its own matched-blank p99 in 6.6–7.3% of local moving-bar samples across both directions and locations, versus about 1% on blank. T5c/d mean absolute Tm9-added impulse modulation was 0.00118–0.00139, above the predeclared 0.0005 minimum, and p90 per-cell voltage modulation was about 0.20. The **partial Tm9→T5 transmission gate passed**. Static bars produced lower source and target modulation. Restoring the settled neural and graded state reproduced a second blank probe exactly; synaptic weights stayed frozen.

Crucially, the **same fixed eight-tick Tm4/Tm9 temporal-order diagnostic that failed in the spike-only arm test now passed its calibration-position screen**. Right-minus-left mean scores for T5c/d were -0.00593/+0.00196 at x=18 and -0.00882/+0.00693 at x=46. All four per-cell median contrasts had the same signs; T5c had 99 and 95 negative cells, and T5d had 66 and 87 positive cells, at the two locations. Contrasts exceeded static-score magnitudes. No direction label selected the cap, lag, weights, or cells: the cap came from blank, the lag was fixed in the prior failed protocol, and local T5 groups came from measured Tm1/Tm2 afferents.

This is a meaningful **subthreshold, local motion cue**, not yet a milestone pass. T5 point-neuron spike contrasts still reverse or vanish between locations, and the order score is a read-only diagnostic rather than a physical T5 compartment or trained decoder. It has not been tested at a withheld position/speed or used by a local learning rule. The next fixed check is x=32 at original and doubled bar speed, without changing the release cap, lag, or score. If it transfers, a minimal local temporal integration mechanism on the measured T5 afferents is justified for a separate opt-in experiment. T4/Mi4 remains unresolved.
