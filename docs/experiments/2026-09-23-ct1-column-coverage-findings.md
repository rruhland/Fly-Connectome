# CT1 partner columns cover the selected T5 fields, but process identity remains unknown

The [read-only column protocol](2026-09-23-ct1-column-coverage-protocol.md) used the original MaleCNS contacts and the existing retina/afferent-derived column mapping. Its [result](2026-09-23-ct1-column-coverage-results.json) reproduces the roster audit's **1,765 Tm1/Tm9→CT1 edges / 32,728 contacts**. For the vertical motion subtypes, CT1→T5c has **19,289 contacts** and CT1→T5d **17,897**; all selected partner mass on these routes could be assigned to a column. The three-contact threshold retains 32,645 input contacts and every T5c/d output contact.

At y=10, y=22, and withheld y=16, **100%** of CT1→T5c/d output contact mass lands in columns where the same CT1 body has a measured Tm1 or Tm9 input, even without allowing a neighboring column. Tm9 alone covers every output column in these three fields; Tm1 alone covers 97.5–98.4% of T5c/d output mass. Thus the predeclared 80% partner-column coverage screen passes, and both source classes are represented at the holdout location.

The screen is permissive because Tm9 inputs to this broad CT1 body cover nearly the entire selected visual field. **Same body and same inferred column do not prove the contacts share one electrically local CT1 branch.** Body-level weights contain no synapse positions, and the T5 column is inferred from Tm1/Tm2 inputs. The [MaleCNS synapse-partner dataset](https://male-cns.janelia.org/download/) has per-contact coordinates that could resolve the process assignment; it is not part of the local raw files. The useful next experiment is therefore explicitly opt-in: split CT1 into column-local shadow compartments, drive them only through these measured Tm1/Tm9 contact counts, and deliver fixed-sign GABA current over the measured CT1→T5 edges. Compare frozen preferred/null T5 responses with CT1 shadow off/on at both locations and the position/speed holdouts. A pass would motivate a synapse-coordinate validation and a concrete architecture discussion, not justify direct production promotion.
