# The simple Tm4/Tm9 order signal is sparse and not subtype selective

The [frozen protocol](2026-09-23-t5-arm-order-protocol.md) separated measured Tm1/Tm2/Tm4/Tm9 feedforward arrivals into T5, using the previously calibrated opt-in Tm4/Tm9 rest currents of 0.85 and matched OFF bar/blank/static probes. The [result](2026-09-23-t5-arm-order-results.json) records graph/checkpoint identities, exact arm-edge counts, location-defined T5c/d groups, source currents, per-cell order scores, and T5 spike excess. No edge or learning parameter changed.

The fixed eight-tick Tm4-before-Tm9 minus reverse-order score **failed** its preregistered screen. Right-minus-left mean scores for T5c/d were both negative at x=18 (-0.000674/-0.000200) and both negative at x=46 (-0.000107/-0.000050). Thus the subtype pair did not oppose one another. Every per-cell median contrast was zero. The mean contrasts exceeded static-score magnitudes, but they were carried by a minority of cells and cannot justify a local timing mechanism on their own. T5c/d OFF spike excess remained substantial but direction differences were only a few spikes and changed pattern between locations.

The arm traces show where the test loses information. Local Tm4 current modulation into T5c/d was approximately 0.003–0.005 under moving bars at both locations. Local Tm9 modulation was only about 0.0005 at x=18 and nearly zero at x=46 (right-moving bars gave exactly zero in the recorded local T5c/d groups). The earlier source audit found a much stronger Tm9 local OFF current residual before its spike-output boundary. Thus the order test is mostly one active arm plus a sparse second arm; its failure does not rule out a T5 temporal interaction **after** effective Tm9 transmission.

A bounded next opt-in test can replace Tm9 spikes with release from its own positive current residual on **all existing Tm9 outgoing edges**, keeping the signs, delays, and contact magnitudes. Calibrate only on blank to prevent saturation, then require Tm9-dependent T5 current at both locations and both directions before repeating the fixed order score. Do not tune lags or weights on direction labels. The T4/Mi4 and M1A gates remain unmet; no production promotion follows from this finding.
