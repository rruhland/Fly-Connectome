# Frozen ON timing and Tm3-column audit

Read-only prerequisite for the [opt-in signal-integration experiment](2026-09-22-motion-stage-signal-integration-proposal.md). The pinned initial graph, weights, signs, delays, event camera, 8 ticks/frame, and 500-tick warm state were unchanged. At each of x=18 and x=46, a 3-by-13 ON bar was flashed for one camera frame or presented at three adjacent columns in right/left order, with a matched blank. Per-tick Mi4 voltage, afferent arrivals, and T4 state were saved to ignored `runs/motion-stage-signal-integration-v1/timing.json`; the versioned [compact summary](2026-09-22-motion-stage-timing-summary.json) includes its SHA-256.

Tm3 lacks direct `assignedOlHex` metadata, but all 1,037 Tm3 cells have measured L1 input suitable for an inferred column. The contact-weighted L1 center uses a median of 76 contacts per Tm3 cell; median axial source spread is 0.755 column units (90th percentile 0.892). This is adequate to assign an approximate center in a diagnostic, **not** a measured dendritic synapse position.

The short probes yielded no T4 spikes above matched blank at either location. Local Mi4 mean-voltage changes were delayed: after the x=18 flash, the most negative difference from blank was -0.00421 at tick 57 (the flash began at tick 16); at x=46 it was -0.00164 at tick 48. Ordered three-position probes also changed Mi4 voltage, but not with a stable, early spike output. This timing cautions against claiming that merely transmitting Mi4's subthreshold state will create next-frame anticipation. It does justify a tightly bounded test of whether the state has any effect on its existing inhibitory T4 edges.

Before viewing motion responses, set the graded experiment's voltage normalization to `0.0042` (rounded maximum absolute local mean change in these **flash-only** probes). The release baseline will be chosen from no-event current so the 99th-percentile added tonic T4 current is at most 0.05 of the unit spike threshold. Test voltage-to-release fractions `0.25, 0.5, 1.0` of that flash scale. Choose the smallest fraction that gives at least 0.005 local T4 current modulation on a flash while keeping finite state and the tonic bound. Motion direction, held-out location, and Pong outcomes must not select a fraction. This is an experimental calibration, not a fitted biological release law.
