# Local trace binding at first-sighting events

The split sensory experiment preserved the learned motion code but could not know direction at reappearance. Its recurrent follow-up maintained activity yet did not causally affect reappearance. This opt-in experiment tests a different local temporal representation: a decaying trace of the **learned** coincidence latent meets raw ON/OFF evidence at sites with no current coincidence. A new competitive local dictionary and local next-event predictive weights are learned from that conjunction. No direction, object, game, or future label enters the model; delayed camera events supply only local prediction error. No backpropagation is used.

Freeze the existing 24-unit learned coincidence population. Maintain its activity trace with fixed per-frame decay 0.8. At a raw-event site lacking current coincidence, sample the trace within an eight-pixel spatial radius and provide its 24 learned-unit channels alongside raw polarity to an eight-unit locally learned context population. The radius is fixed before training from the frozen disappearance suite: radius two reaches 36/116 reappearance sites, radius four 70/116, radius six 94/116, and radius eight 116/116. Spatial pooling is a low-level access primitive; the learned context units remain unassigned and the final code is not a fixed motion bank. Train the context dictionary with two passes and its local delayed next-event weights with one pass over the same 96 clean plus 32 generic interruption/noise episodes used previously.

Controls: the archived coincidence-only online predictor, an identically trained eight-unit context population with its history trace always zero, and the trained trace model with its trace reset just before t=10. Evaluate their local event forecasts at threshold 0.5 on unseen single patterns, all five robust families, exact t=10→11 reappearance, quiet-target false alarms, and matched left/right histories with identical current input. Count whether trace-bearing context codes differ across those matched histories. The context prediction may supplement the archived predictor through a fixed pixelwise maximum; report both where relevant.

The primary gate is at least 0.10 absolute F1 improvement on exact reappearance over **both** zero-trace and reset controls, with different forecasts for the matched histories. Clean single-pattern F1 should stay within 0.03 of the archived learner and quiet false alarms below twice its count. If only an offline or training score improves, do not claim online learning. A failed run ends this variant; do not sweep trace decay, radius, units, or threshold here. Production M1A remains unchanged.
