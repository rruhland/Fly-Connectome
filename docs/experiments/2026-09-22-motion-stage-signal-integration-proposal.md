# Proposed next opt-in motion-circuit experiment: graded inhibition, then local conductance

Status: **proposal for review, not implemented**. The approved [rest-current recovery](2026-09-22-motion-stage-recovery-findings.md) made two anatomically present OFF-pathway arms active but did not produce location-stable T5 tuning; Mi4 remained spike-silent despite a repeatable subthreshold visual signal. The pinned graph preserves opposite Mi9/Mi4 source-column offsets across T4 subtypes. These observations motivate testing whether missing graded release and local conductance integration, rather than another predictive plasticity tweak, prevent the graph from expressing motion.

## Constraints and order

The original signed, delayed edges and contact counts remain fixed. No backprop, trained direction labels, new connectivity, global weight tying, or model-wide gain optimization. Work is opt-in and benchmarked against the frozen current baseline, with matching blanks and the same event camera/8 ticks per frame. Keep the fast spiking path unchanged for other cell classes; process only the measured motion-input edges added to this trial in vectorized batches. Do not resume full-Pong M1A until a frozen motion stage passes the tests below.

1. **Measure before modifying:** Record per-tick local Mi4 voltage, Mi9/Mi1/Tm3 arrivals, and T4 state for ON flashes and ordered adjacent-column events at two positions. Infer Tm3's missing column metadata from its measured L1 afferents, checking coverage and uncertainty. This uses no changed dynamics and verifies the actual temporal ordering in the pinned graph.
2. **Opt-in graded Mi4 transmission:** On existing Mi4→T4 edges only, replace the all-or-none release event with a bounded nonnegative release level driven by each presynaptic Mi4 cell's own membrane voltage relative to a locally maintained no-event operating point. A hyperpolarization reduces GABAergic release; it never makes the synapse excitatory. Preserve each edge's anatomical weight and delay. Select a minimal release scale from blank and single-flash responses alone, with a predeclared low blank-current and finite-activity bound. Compare against the original spiking Mi4 control and a voltage-shuffled control. Stop if the small observed voltage modulation cannot yield stimulus-dependent target current without excessive tonic conductance.
3. **Only if signal reaches T4 but direction still fails:** Use the existing source-column offsets to assign T4 afferents to proximal/central/distal *local* compartments, with passive coupling and inhibitory/excitatory reversal potentials. Mi1/Tm3 supply the central excitatory arm, Mi9 and Mi4 retain their measured inhibitory signs on their offset arms. This tests the experimentally supported conductance/disinhibition interaction while preserving all source-target edges. The column-offset assignment is an approximation because this dataset does not retain individual synapse coordinates; record that limitation for review. Keep separate ablations for graded release alone, compartments alone, and both. Do not invent an independently trained gate.
4. **Extend to T5 only after the T4 mechanism is understood:** Use measured Tm1/Tm2/Tm4/Tm9 inputs and their temporal/column offsets, with their fixed transmitter identities. Do not copy T4 parameters to T5 without evidence.

Frozen checks require T4 ON and T5 OFF activity above matched blanks, low static/quiet responses, and subtype-specific direction preferences that recur at two calibration positions plus a held-out position and speed. A responder must have at least ten evoked spikes across opposed directions and a direction index `|right-left|/(right+left) >= 0.2`; report per-cell distributions and opposite-subtype comparisons as well as the summary index. If the spiking output remains too sparse, use membrane and compartment currents as diagnostics only, not as a substitute pass. No parameter may be selected on held-out motion or Pong score. If the opt-in circuit passes, the **separate** learning proposal will shift local prediction credit toward motion-stage feedforward activity on existing predictive edges and require online held-out-location improvement before full Pong.

Biological basis: [measured T4 input signs](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0896627317301939), [T4 passive conductance and disinhibition physiology](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-022-04428-3), and [T5 contrast-opponent inputs](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-021-24986-w). The proposed release law and compartment approximation are **model hypotheses**, not quantities directly measured by this connectome. Their value is determined by the frozen, position-held-out tests, not plausibility alone.
