# Frozen correlation-input latent robustness suite

Retrain the successful opt-in correlation-input latent circuit from the same seed and 96 diverse, unlabeled training episodes, with no new fitting on test scenes. Confirm that its original 48 single-pattern held-out event F1 reproduces within 0.01 of the archived 0.669 result. Then evaluate next-frame ON/OFF event F1 on new connected patterns in five generic scene families: two spatially separated independent movers, two movers crossing, a mover briefly disappearing and reappearing, a mover changing between one and two pixels/frame, and sensor-bit noise. Shapes are diamond/ring/zigzag, not used to train the dictionary. No object labels, tracks, velocities, or scenario names enter the model.

Compare the learned dictionary/local predictor to a matched random dictionary with locally trained prediction and the fixed event-correlation sensory forecaster. Score the same t=3..13 horizon and threshold 0.5, aggregated by family and speed where applicable. For speed changes, separately score target frames at the rate switch. For disappearances, separately score transition frames; those may be intrinsically unpredictable from local evidence. Record runtime. A useful next milestone requires at least 0.50 learned event F1 on two independent movers and speed changes and a 0.20 margin over the random dictionary. The other families diagnose the limits rather than serve as pass/fail gates. This is an opt-in evaluation, not production integration.
