Report support when the sum of absolute current and delayed arm residuals exceeds 0.001, the two individual-arm support fractions, and the precomputed local order/current gate support (>0.05). This is an information/availability screen, not evidence that a new plasticity rule learns. If it fails, do not train the afferent rule yet; identify which source arm or timescale is missing.
