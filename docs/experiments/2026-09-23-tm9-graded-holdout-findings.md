# T5 temporal-order signs transfer to withheld position and speed; occupancy gate narrowly fails

The [held-out protocol](2026-09-23-tm9-graded-holdout-protocol.md) froze the Tm9 source-current rule, blank-selected cap 0.10, existing measured edges, and eight-tick Tm4/Tm9 order score. It tested x=32 at one and two pixels/frame with matched bright blanks, opposite-direction event-count controls, and a static bar. The [result](2026-09-23-tm9-graded-holdout-results.json) records per-cell T5c/d contrasts and target/source metrics. Both directions had 312 pixel events at speed one and 624 at speed two; no direction received more sensor evidence.

The **order cue transferred cleanly**. At x=32 speed one, right-minus-left mean scores were -0.00865 for T5c and +0.00357 for T5d, with per-cell medians -0.00621/+0.00160. At speed two they were -0.01114/+0.00839, with medians -0.00904/+0.00722. Each contrast was much larger than its same-subtype static-score magnitude (at most 0.00066). The sign matched the two calibration positions without changing the lag, release cap, source-to-edge mapping, or local-cell rule. T5c had 143/173 cells with negative contrasts and T5d 124/150 with positive contrasts at the two speeds. This supports a location- and speed-transferable **local subthreshold motion-order state** in the opt-in measured graph.

The **predeclared held-out screen nevertheless failed** its source-occupancy part. Local Tm9 release exceeded matched-blank p99 in 6.33%/6.16% of right/left samples at speed one and 5.33%/4.17% at speed two; the last value is below the required 5%. All four target added-impulse modulations exceeded 0.0005 for both T5c and T5d, and the order signs, medians, and static comparisons passed. The faster trajectory spends fewer of the fixed 96 transit ticks inside the unchanged ±8-pixel local window, which is a plausible explanation for the lower occupancy; it was **not** used to alter the gate after the fact.

T5 spike direction contrasts are still unstable: T5c changed from 120 right versus 130 left spikes at speed one to 145 right versus 115 left at speed two, while T5d was nearly balanced at speed one. The temporal-order score is an **offline readout of local currents**, not yet a physical dendritic/current mechanism or online learned prediction. T4 remains silent. The evidence now justifies a bounded opt-in T5 postsynaptic temporal-integration experiment with anatomically defined local arms, a blank-calibrated gain, and no trained direction label. Production promotion and M1A acceptance remain blocked until that mechanism produces transferable spiking or locally learnable activity and the T4 path is separately addressed.
