# Read-only voltage audit of LC11's silent inhibitory afferents

The measured right LC11 inputs include 55,059 inhibitory contacts, but major inhibitory source classes did not spike under x=18 dot or blank probes. Before another graded-release model, test whether those cells' *existing membrane voltages* contain an inhibitory context signal.

Fix the ten largest inhibitory input classes by measured contact mass from the [signed-input audit](2026-09-23-lc11-signed-input-results.json): Li15, MeLo10, Li26, TmY19a, TmY19b, MeLo12, Li25, MeLo8, TmY15, and Y14. Keep the pinned initial network, 500-tick warm/reset, 8 ticks/frame, and plasticity off. At x=18 and x=46, replay ON/OFF right-moving dot and bar and static-dot onset/offset controls versus matched blanks. Record each source's 144-tick voltage and spikes; use only its raw measured **outgoing LC11 contact count** to weight read-only summaries. There is no new current or release mechanism.

For each class/condition, report source-neuron count and contact mass, mean absolute and signed voltage difference from matched blank during frames 3–14, contact-weighted signed and absolute difference, and number of cells whose time-averaged absolute difference exceeds 0.002. Report dot versus bar contrasts at both positions, with event counts. A candidate for graded surround inhibition should show reproducible positive voltage modulation in at least five cells of the *same* inhibitory class at both positions for at least one polarity, and its contact-weighted signed response to the bar should exceed its dot response by at least 0.002 at both positions. If none does, stop the LC11 graded-inhibition branch; adding release would amplify background without demonstrated context selectivity. This is a diagnostic criterion, not a biological threshold or trained decoder.
