# Separate local error for observed and imagined emission synapses

The first two-path circuit applied one combined visible-event prediction error to both observed-source and imagined-source emission banks. Its recurrent arm underfit, and deleting imagined emission at evaluation made it worse. Run one bounded opt-in credit change: when the next observed ON/OFF event image arrives, update each bank from its **own** local prediction residual (`target − observed-bank prediction` or `target − imagined-bank prediction`) at its existing eligible source synapses. Preserve the learned dictionary, latent transition rule, source selection, 128-episode training corpus, combined emitted forecast, rates, seeds, and frozen evaluation suite. No branch receives a motion, object, visibility, or game label.

Compare this separated-credit recurrent arm with the saved shared-credit recurrent and no-recurrence emission arms, the matched primitive learner, archived learner, and fixed sensory control. Report training and held-out event F1 by family, quiet-target false-alarm pixels, and blank-input latent persistence. Support for further development requires training F1 at least within 0.03 of no-recurrence emission, a 0.05 held-out gain on disappearance or noise over both matched arms, no more than 0.03 loss on clean single and independent motion, and no more than 20% extra quiet false alarms over the archived learner. If it fails, stop tuning this two-bank recurrent design and revisit the latent transition/state objective. Production M1A remains unchanged.
