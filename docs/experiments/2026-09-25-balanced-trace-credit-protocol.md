# Separate local event-confirming and quiet-suppressing credit

The online continuous-trace learner emits no correct gap-exit events at the fixed threshold and its held-out event/quiet AUC degrades from 0.814 at two-frame gaps to 0.614 at four-frame gaps. A frozen shared trace decoder preserves a higher 0.907 pooled AUC even after quiet examples shrink its amplitude. Test one bounded local credit revision before adding another temporal state.

Keep the saved learned motion code, continuous decaying trace, zero-initialized 17×17 ON/OFF emission field, one-pass 288-episode corpus, and 0.5 scoring threshold. At each previously active trace source, split delayed local error into (1) event-confirming current when an event arrives and (2) quiet-suppressing current where this synapse predicted activity without an event. Normalize each current by its own locally observed active sites in that source's field, then apply the same learning rate 0.5 and [0,1] weight bounds. This uses only presynaptic trace, local target events, and local prior prediction; no backpropagation, global label, direction channel, object model, or Pong-specific signal.

Train an otherwise identical **shuffled-credit** control by pairing each sensory episode's local trace with next-event targets from a different generic episode at the same time index. Evaluate the revised learner, shuffled control, original shared-error learner, zero-weight and trace-reset controls, archived correlation-input predictor, and fixed raw-event control on training gaps, held-out unseen-shape two-/three-/four-frame gap exits, full generic scenes, and quiet targets. Report fixed-threshold F1 and threshold-free event/quiet AUC, including each gap duration.

Count this as a meaningful online credit improvement only if familiar and early held-out gap-exit F1 each reach at least 0.20, late reaches at least 0.10, each exceeds shuffled credit by 0.10, and fixed-plus-learned full-scene F1 does not regress by more than 0.03 on any non-occlusion family or exceed twice fixed raw's quiet false alarms. If it fails, stop adjusting this wide passive-trace emitter and move to a learned recurrent transition with separate hidden-state and event-emission credit. This opt-in experiment changes no in-place production architecture.
