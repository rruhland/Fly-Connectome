# Frozen T5 motion transmission into LPi learning targets

The measured LPi34 and LPi43 populations receive respectively 47,281 T5c and 20,628 T5d contacts, as well as local predictive afferents. Test whether the **frozen opt-in T5 timing current** transmits its vertical direction signal across those existing edges before learning at LPi. Keep the graph, checkpoint, signs, delays, Tm9 release, T5 gate, and eight ticks/frame fixed. Replay up/down moving OFF bars, matched bright blank and static bar at y=10/y=22, plus y=16 at one and two pixels/frame; opposite directions must have equal camera events. Include a gate-off comparator from the same blank-calibrated state.

For LPi34, LPi43, and mixed LPi3412, report per-cell T5c/d spike-derived feedforward impulse, **total** captured feedforward-arrival target, postsynaptic spikes, and recurrent predictive current. The T5-specific impulse must preserve the measured positive T5 sign. LPi34 should inherit the T5c negative down-minus-up contrast, and LPi43 the T5d positive contrast at both calibration locations and withheld speeds. The total local feedforward target must retain each sign, with magnitude at least half the corresponding T5-specific contrast; gate-off T5-specific contrasts must be less than half the gate-on contrast at the withheld center. Per-cell majorities should agree with the population sign. A pass identifies a real motion-bearing local target with measured predictive inputs; it is not evidence of online learning. Stop if the signal vanishes or is canceled before LPi.
