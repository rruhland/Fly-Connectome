# One bounded revision: predict evidence over local fields

The [site-local visibility head](2026-09-25-local-visibility-protocol.md) improves next-event Brier at individual active sites but miscalibrates intrinsically uncertain gap exits. The [feature audit](2026-09-25-visibility-feature-audit-results.json) shows why: even when an event returns somewhere in the moving field, the fraction of exact candidate sites with a nearby event decreases with time since evidence; the four-frame state also falls below the 0.5 site threshold in some cases.

Test one different credit unit while keeping the frozen locally learned hidden transition. Pool continuous hidden state, its carried elapsed-evidence value, and current sensory evidence in overlapping 16×16 retinotopic fields at stride 8. A single shared local probability unit learns whether any event appears in the same field on the next frame. Train with delayed local Bernoulli error; no field knows the scene label, gap length, shape, or object identity. Compare a time-shuffled-credit head and empirical fixed field hazard. Evaluate on unseen shapes and positions, checking exact equality on matched visual prefixes. Report field Brier, global any-event Brier and quiet-frame Brier, pair probability/Brier, and how often positive events lack any eligible field.

Advance to a signed-event head only if this bounded revision improves overall and quiet Brier over fixed hazard, has matched-pair Brier at most 0.27 (irreducible minimum 0.25), and retains eligible field coverage of at least 90% of gap-exit event pixels. Otherwise stop visibility tuning and reassess the factorization, especially how hidden-state survival and evidence uncertainty should interact. Production architecture remains unchanged.
