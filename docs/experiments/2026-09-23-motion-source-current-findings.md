# Current-state audit does not rescue the missing Mi4 timing signal

The [preregistered frozen screen](2026-09-23-motion-source-current-protocol.md) replayed matched moving/static ON/OFF bars at x=18 and x=46 from the original 500-tick warm checkpoint. The [result](2026-09-23-motion-source-current-results.json) records checkpoint/graph identities, annotated local and opposite-location cell groups, per-cell summaries, blank floors, and candidate peak ticks. No dynamics, connectivity, sign, weight, or plasticity changed.

Mi4's **negative voltage residual** passed the source-information screen for ON bars at both locations: 5.3%/10.2% of local samples exceeded the cell's blank p99, versus about 1% on blank. Its positive and negative **current** residuals did **not** pass at both locations (negative current reached 4.0%/3.6%, below the 5% criterion). The voltage result is consistent with the prior graded Mi4 pilot, which already failed to generate enough flash-dependent T4 current under its blank-current limit. Moreover, the strongest positive change in the negative-voltage candidate occurred at ticks 137 and 129, after the transit window ended at tick 120. This audit therefore gives **no evidence to repeat a Mi4 release-gain sweep**; its small and delayed cue remains the limiting concern.

Tm4 and Tm9 each passed several local residual screens for the OFF bar. Their positive current residuals exceeded matched-blank p99 in 19.6%/19.3% of local Tm4 samples and 9.3%/8.7% of local Tm9 samples at x=18/x=46, versus about 1% on blank. Tm4 mean absolute local current-residual modulation was 0.117/0.102 and Tm9 0.0238/0.0181, much larger than their opposite-location changes. This is source information, not direction selectivity. The earlier rest-current pilot already turned both classes into stimulus-dependent spikers, yet T5 preference still reversed or vanished across positions. Simply making more of the same source activity reach a point-sum T5 is therefore not a demonstrated solution.

The approved architecture amendment remains at its feature gate. The next bounded motion experiment should examine **when and where** the measured T5 arm currents arrive, with subtype- and afferent-defined local groups, opposite directions, and static controls. The question is whether a fixed local temporal/conductance interaction could express a transferable OFF preference from signals already present, before any T5 compartment or local-learning change is implemented. Mi4 needs a separately justified physiology/transduction hypothesis rather than another scalar output calibration.
