# Does online local credit preserve trace-based event ranking?

The continuous-trace learner scores zero gap-exit true positives at the fixed 0.5 threshold, even on training scenes. A frozen offline readout trained on gap-exit plus quiet examples also scores zero at that threshold, yet still ranks held-out future-event pixels above reachable quiet pixels with AUC 0.907. Before altering local plasticity or output gain, measure whether the **online-trained** weights preserve any comparable ranking.

Retrain the exact same one-pass 288-episode learner from `scripts/online_blank_trace.py` without changing weights, code, learning rate, field, or data. Save its experimental checkpoint under `runs/` for reproducible follow-up. On the 192 training interruptions and the unchanged unseen-shape three-frame/early-two-frame/late-four-frame suites, collect raw output scores at last-blank-frame → reappearance target pixels and at local-trace-reachable pixels on the immediately preceding quiet-target frame. Report event and quiet score means/quantiles, fixed-threshold F1, and threshold-free event-versus-quiet ROC AUC by gap duration. Compare with the offline shared readout's 0.907 held-out AUC; the zero-weight output is a 0.5-ranking control.

If online held-out AUC is at least 0.80 across the gaps, test a locally computed activity/rate calibration or balanced local plasticity mechanism in a separate experiment. If AUC is weak despite the trace's offline ranking, redesign online eligibility/update credit before adding recurrent complexity. Do not choose a new decision threshold from these results. No production architecture change.
