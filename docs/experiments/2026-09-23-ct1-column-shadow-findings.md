# Measured CT1 contacts modestly suppress T5, without reliable null selectivity

The [preregistered opt-in CT1 shadow](2026-09-23-ct1-column-shadow-protocol.md) used the unchanged checkpoint and T5 local-order experiment. Its only added current came from **1,715 measured threshold-three Tm1/Tm9→CT1 edges (32,645 contacts)** and **3,355 measured CT1→T5 edges (80,977 contacts)**, with fixed positive Tm1/Tm9 and negative GABA CT1 signs. Each inferred column was electrically isolated from the other CT1 shadow columns. A causal two-leg test verified that a source event could not reach T5 early or leak across columns. Both arms used the same additional CT1 blank-calibration state; there was no training or parameter sweep.

The [frozen result](2026-09-23-ct1-column-shadow-results.json) shows a **small physical effect**: activating CT1 reduced T5c null-direction spikes by **3.1%, 7.3%, 5.6%, and 8.1%** across y=10/y=22 and withheld y=16 speeds one/two. T5d null-direction reductions were **5.9%, 3.4%, 3.2%, and 3.3%**. Preferred-direction retention stayed above 93%, direction preference retained its sign, and both blank groups stayed silent. Yet none of eight subtype/condition comparisons reached the predeclared 10% null-reduction threshold. More critically, CT1 current was not consistently stronger during null motion: the mean null/preferred difference reversed for T5c at y=22 and T5d at y=10 and y=16/speed one. Tm9-driven local release occupied about 8–12% of tested column-ticks in motion and was nearly direction-symmetric. The fixed-profile rescue screen therefore **fails**.

This does not invalidate the measured CT1 pathway or the physiology. The body-to-body data identify partner neurons and contact counts, but the shadow had to assign each CT1 input to its source's column and each output to its T5 target's inferred column. That approximation cannot recover the actual local branch and spatial offset of the Tm1/Tm9→CT1→T5 motif. The [official synapse-partner table](https://male-cns.janelia.org/download/) contains the per-contact coordinates needed to test that geometry. The result argues against promoting a whole-cell CT1 or this simple column shadow into production. The next investigation should recover CT1's contact geometry, then ask whether a measured local inhibitory arm gives a consistent null-biased, early current before changing any in-place architecture or plasticity rule.
