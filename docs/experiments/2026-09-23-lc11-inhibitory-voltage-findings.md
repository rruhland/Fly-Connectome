# LC11's major inhibitory sources lack a transferable subthreshold surround signal

The [read-only protocol](2026-09-23-lc11-inhibitory-voltage-protocol.md) measured 451 source neurons carrying 42,218 of the 55,059 inhibitory contacts from pinned M1 cells into traced right LC11. Frozen dot, bar, and static-dot probes at x=18 and x=46 used matched ON/OFF blanks and never changed the model. The [results](2026-09-23-lc11-inhibitory-voltage-results.json) contain checksums, contact-weighted voltage responses, source-cell counts, and spike counts.

No class met the preset same-class, both-location, bar-above-dot inhibitory-context screen. The two heaviest inhibitory classes were particularly uninformative: Li15 (8,175 LC11 contacts) had **zero** measured voltage modulation and spikes in every tested condition; MeLo10 (7,044 contacts) was zero or approximately zero. TmY19a had a substantial bar response at x=18 (contact-weighted signed voltage +0.00355 ON, +0.01102 OFF; 16/20 positive cells above 0.002), but the same class was slightly **negative** at x=46 (-0.00047 ON, -0.00038 OFF; 0/3 positive cells). MeLo8 was also negative for the x=46 OFF bar (-0.00409), which would reduce, not increase, its inhibitory output under a monotonic voltage-to-release rule. None of these major classes spiked in the tested bar conditions.

The current spiking implementation therefore has neither effective inhibitory spikes nor a simple location-stable, bar-preferring voltage signal in its largest inhibitory LC11 afferents. A graded-release law on those cells cannot be assumed to restore biological surround suppression. This explains why adding all signed measured LC11 inputs had almost no effect on the partial readout. It does **not** show that the fly connectome lacks the computation: its measured contacts and primary physiology implicate local inhibition; the simulator's current sensory and cell dynamics do not express it.

Stop the LC11 gain, rest-current, and simple graded-output branch here. The next useful work is a **reviewable architecture design** for retinotopic, cell-class-specific visual dynamics and local dendritic integration, with one small controlled acceptance test before returning to M1A learning. Preserve measured topology, fixed transmitter signs, local plasticity, and no backprop. The positive T2/T3 voltage result means object information is present upstream, but this audit shows it cannot simply be summed across silent inhibitory inputs to make a reliable detector.
