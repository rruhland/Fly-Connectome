# Exact blank subtraction does not rescue the next-frame afferent target

The [paired-blank upper-bound test](2026-09-23-t5-paired-afferent-protocol.md) subtracted each frozen trial's **same-cell, same-tick bright-blank physical arrival trace** before thresholding future feedforward increments. This is stronger information than an online cell-local baseline could normally provide. It changed no graph edge, sign, delay, neural state equation, release law, T5 gate, or weight. The [result](2026-09-23-t5-paired-afferent-results.json) fails the predeclared screen on all six position/speed cases.

For preferred moving cases, event/quiet ROC AUC improves slightly over the raw target but reaches only **0.602–0.641** at one pixel/frame and **0.516–0.538** at two pixels/frame, against the required 0.70. Event-order means are at least twice quiet at the slow speed, but nearly overlap at the fast speed. Paired future-arrival events still occupy **28–34%** of moving samples and **19–28%** of static samples; static prevalence is not below half of preferred motion. Thus even an exact counterfactual blank trace does not isolate a sufficiently specific next-frame physical-arrival target for this T5 state.

Do not implement a causal baseline merely to mimic this failed upper bound, and do not train afferent weights against this target. The 50 ms opt-in Tm4 profile remains a successful **frozen motion feature**, not a learned forecast. The next target diagnostic should test future **motion-selective T5 spiking** against current spike persistence and static/blank controls, or a longer biological horizon, before any local credit rule is written. A future-spike target is local but must be checked for trivial persistence because T5's current order gate contributes to its own spikes. Production architecture and learning remain unchanged.
