# T5 order transfer to withheld position and speed

The [bounded Tm9 pilot](2026-09-23-tm9-graded-findings.md) passed its partial transmission and fixed eight-tick T5c/d order screens at x=18 and x=46 with one cap selected only from blank. This follow-up **freezes** that source-current equation, Tm4/Tm9 rest profile, cap-selection procedure, measured graph, eight-tick lag, arm score, and local T5c/d grouping rule. No direction label or held-out observation changes a parameter.

Replay the same matched bright blank, moving OFF bars in both directions, and static OFF bar at **x=32**, first at one pixel/frame and then at **two pixels/frame**. Use the same 13 active frames in an 18-frame episode and eight neural ticks/frame. A speed-two bar moves 12 pixels to either side of its center; keep the same ±8-pixel anatomical local-cell window to avoid changing the readout footprint to fit the trajectory. Record source release, graded target current, per-cell T5c/d order scores, static scores, and spike excess. Confirm opposite directions have equal event counts at each speed.

The held-out screen requires at **both** speeds: Tm9 source release above matched-blank p99 in at least 5% of local samples, T5c/d mean absolute added-impulse modulation at least 0.0005, T5c right-minus-left mean order score negative and T5d positive (the signs fixed by the two calibration positions), each subtype's per-cell median with the same sign as its mean, and each contrast larger in magnitude than its static-score mean. A pass establishes an opt-in, location/speed-transferable *subthreshold* temporal cue. It does not establish T5 spike direction tuning or M1A learning and does not promote any production architecture.
