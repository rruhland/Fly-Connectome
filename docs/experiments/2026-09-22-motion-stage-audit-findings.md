# Where does motion become decodable in the current optic-lobe simulation?

Status: frozen, read-only diagnostic; no learning or production dynamics changed.
Protocol: [motion-stage audit](2026-09-22-motion-stage-audit-protocol.md). The initial `event-v1-combined-rate` full-graph checkpoint, sensor, fixed signs, delays, weights, 8 ticks/frame, and 500-tick warm state were unchanged. Each trial restored the same warm state. OFF trials used a bright matched blank; ON trials used a dark matched blank. The stored per-neuron response files are in ignored `runs/motion-stage-audit-v1/`; the six compact JSON reports beside this file are versioned. Their source checkpoint and annotation SHA-256 values are recorded in the reports.

## What the frozen graph expresses

The radius-1.5 dot and the preregistered 3-by-13 bar traversed identical 13-position paths to the right or left, centered at x=18 or x=46. Opposing directions had the same stimulus occupancy and event count at each location (dot: 72 pixel events; bar: 312). ON and OFF were also event-count matched. The bar generated 684 nonzero sensory-neuron injections at x=18 and 699 at x=46. Thus direction differences here are not caused by one direction receiving more camera events.

Measured lamina cells respond: for the x=18 ON-right bar, local L1/L2/L3 transit spikes were 228/255/156. But the stage where the fly is expected to first express direction selectivity does not work in this simulation. T4a-d each produced **zero local transit spikes** for every dot and bar direction, polarity, and location; their matched blanks were also zero. The subtype-local window is inferred from each T4 neuron's direct Mi1 afferents; runtime retinal columns only label sensory neurons. For the bar, each local T4 subtype group included 51–65 neurons. The inferred receptive column is a contact-weighted centroid, so this is an approximation rather than dendritic reconstruction.

T5 does respond mainly to OFF motion, but it does not show a robust local direction code in these probes. For the OFF bar, local right-minus-left spike contrasts for T5a/b/c/d were `[-1,+3,+3,+1]` at x=18 and `[+3,+2,+3,+3]` at x=46. The apparent consistency for b/c/d is only 1–3 extra spikes in groups of roughly 50–70 cells, while T5a reverses; it is not enough to claim transferable direction selectivity. For ON, b and d reverse between positions. The dot responses were still weaker. These are fixed-probe observations, not a fitted decoder or a claim that no downstream representation can ever be extracted.

T4 has a small subthreshold direction difference, but not an obvious subtype-specific one. For the ON bar, mean local right-minus-left membrane voltage at x=18 was `+0.0022` to `+0.0027` across T4a-d; at x=46 it was `-0.0007` to `-0.0011` across all four. A simple T4 threshold shift is therefore unlikely, by itself, to expose a location-stable motion code.

## Where the signal stalls

The pinned graph *does* retain the relevant measured connections. For example, T4a receives 1,120 Mi4 edges (8,562 contacts), and T5a receives 1,577 Tm4 edges (13,134 contacts) and 2,562 Tm9 edges (26,752 contacts). Yet Mi4, Tm4, and Tm9 each fired **zero spikes globally** during blank, ON, and OFF bar probes. Their mean membrane voltage remained far below the unit threshold (largest ON mean across cells: Mi4 0.012, Tm4 0.266, Tm9 0.078 at x=18). They have no class-specific resting current in `resting-v2-motion-provisional`, unlike Mi1, Tm3, Tm1, Tm2, Mi9, T4, and T5. Consequently their anatomically present synapses contributed no arrivals to local T4/T5 in the bar afferent audit.

The active T4 inputs also move the wrong way for a simple ON-spiking response. At x=18, the local T4 population received 647 Mi1 and 196 Tm3 arrivals on blank, versus 514 and 192 for the ON-right bar; its positive summed arrival magnitude fell from 61.58 to 50.86. Mi9 arrivals rose from 631 to 778, and negative summed arrival magnitude grew from -29.91 to -36.79. At x=46, positive magnitude likewise fell 34.13 to 26.79 and negative magnitude grew -45.03 to -58.68. These are summed incoming impulses, **not** membrane currents or a direction decoder; the distinction is important. T4 local spikes stayed at 2 versus 2 for x=18 and x=46 in the broader afferent window. Meanwhile T5 receives Tm1/Tm2 arrivals and responds to OFF bars (x=18: 90 right, 89 left; x=46: 70 right, 60 left in the broader window), but no Tm4/Tm9 arrivals.

The lamina sensor injects the same signed `OFF − ON` event-camera contrast into L1, L2, and L3. This is not automatically biologically wrong: L1's direct response can have an OFF sign while its inhibitory connection disinhibits the ON-pathway Mi1/Tm3. In the graph, L1→Mi1/Tm3 edges are negative, whereas L3→Mi1/Mi9 and L2→Tm1/Tm2/Tm4 edges are positive. The *net* simulated Mi1/Tm3 and Mi9 responses above show that the present sensor-to-cell dynamics still need a pathway-level polarity/temporal audit before changing input routing. Relabeling L1 as ON by fiat would not be justified.

## Implication for M1A

Fly optic-lobe anatomy uses repeated retinotopic motifs, and physiology identifies T4 and T5 dendrites as the first direction-selective ON and OFF stages, respectively; spatially offset and temporally distinct inputs are essential ([T4 input physiology](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0896627317301939), [T4/T5 receptive fields](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27488629/), [T5 contrast opponency](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-021-24986-w), [measured wiring](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-08746-0)). The simulation currently preserves many anatomical edges but fails to *express* several of those input arms. A different learning rule on the 17,557 predictive edges ending at directly observed L1/L2/L3 cells cannot directly tune T4/T5 synapses; this helps explain why a locally successful precursor rule did not transfer to full Pong. It does not prove that downstream plasticity alone would solve M1A.

The biologically grounded order of hypotheses is:

1. **Input-class dynamics/temporal filtering:** Mi4, Tm4, and Tm9 are silent because the uniform spiking approximation lacks an appropriate operating point or graded release. First test whether a minimal, class-specific operating-point calibration restores stimulus-dependent activity and arrivals without high blank firing. Mi4/Mi9 are reported as slower than Mi1/Tm3; Tm9 contributes temporally distinct OFF-pathway information ([T4 physiology](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0896627317301939), [T5 contrast study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-021-24986-w)). Rest current alone may fail; do not assume it is the full mechanism.
2. **Pathway polarity and spatial alignment:** With active afferents, test impulse responses and paired ON/OFF traversals from L1/L2/L3 through Mi1/Tm3/Mi9 and Tm1/Tm2/Tm4/Tm9 to T4/T5 at several positions. If the measured signed input pattern still suppresses the expected T4 ON response, investigate class-specific sensory transduction and retinotopic alignment as a separate, reviewable change. Preserve fixed anatomical transmitter signs.
3. **Learning target:** Only after the motion stage responds reliably, test local prediction of its feedforward arrivals or local T4/T5 activity on existing predictive edges. Keep causal eligibility, local error, no backprop, and measured topology. Compare position-held-out direction/event prediction against persistence and an untrained frozen control. The current L1/L2/L3 next-frame objective can remain a sensory baseline, but should not be treated as the sole route to useful motion features.
4. **Dendritic representation:** If active, correctly signed inputs still do not create subtype-specific direction tuning, a point-neuron sum may be inadequate. Spatially organized dendritic integration is then the larger architectural question, rather than more scalar plasticity sweeps.

The repeated motif is analogous to convolution in *structure*, but this implementation does not share trained weights across columns. Earlier Pong exposure showed hundreds of L1 cells receive fewer than 10 events even after 10,000 frames ([exposure audit](2026-09-19-local-signals-and-exposure.md)). Any claim of screen-wide online generalization must therefore be tested on held-out locations, and any weight-sharing proposal must explain how it remains local and biologically defensible.

The next bounded experiment and its stop conditions are specified in [motion-stage recovery proposal](2026-09-22-motion-stage-recovery-proposal.md). The diagnostic does not pass M1A and makes no claim of learned prediction.
