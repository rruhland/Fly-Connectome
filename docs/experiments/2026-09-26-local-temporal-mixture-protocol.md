# Locally selected temporal transition pathways

**Status:** Completed opt-in architectural experiment after the [multi-cadence retention failure](2026-09-26-multicadence-experience-findings.md); see [findings](2026-09-26-local-temporal-mixture-findings.md). Production M1A is unchanged.

Freeze two locally learned distributed transition pathways: the original 64-scene slow/general field and the 256-scene multi-cadence field. Share the original locally learned event observer between them. At each visual site and ON/OFF channel, a small local selector reads the two pathways' nearby continuous sensory/recurrent state and mixes their two future-event predictions. Train selector synapses on unlabeled multi-cadence streams using only their local source activity and the difference between each pathway's **local** next-event prediction errors. Do not propagate gradients through either pathway, use a tempo/scene/domain label, or use a hand-coded rule that assigns slow versus fast input to a pathway. The pathways are frozen during selector learning. The two training distributions are an explicit bounded test of whether simultaneous locally learned temporal degrees of freedom can retain both behaviors; self-organized pathway acquisition would remain future work.

Compare learned aligned selector, fixed equal mixture, and a selector trained with time-shuffled local credit. Score the same held-out changed-motion, clean/noisy generic, and both raw Pong camera-cadence groups against the original and multi-cadence single pathways. The gate sees only local visual state; future targets are withheld until local credit/scoring. Keep pathway, observer, selector rates and visual evidence cadence fixed after registration.

**Decision:** The mixture is useful only if it retains at least 90% of the original pathway's clean/noisy generic and native-Pong top-32 recall, at least 90% of the multi-cadence pathway's changed-position/speed/multiple-entity top-32 recall, and beats fixed-equal and shuffled selector credit in aggregate. If it cannot jointly retain regimes, stop this transition-family extension and reconsider the recurrent temporal representation instead of tuning the gate.
