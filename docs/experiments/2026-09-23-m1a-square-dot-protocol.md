# Same-shape four-direction motion probe

The bar comparison's offline four-way decoding could exploit bar orientation: vertical motion used a horizontal bar, and horizontal motion used a vertical bar. This opt-in follow-up removes that cue by using one 3×3 dark square for up/down/left/right. Keep the frozen network, source checkpoint, graph, signs, delays, all previously calibrated current and rest parameters, eight neural ticks/frame, and the event camera unchanged. No long-term weights update.

Calibrate vertical OFF at y=10/22 and horizontal OFF at x=18/46, speed 1. Withhold vertical y=14/18 at speeds 1 and 2, and horizontal x=32 at speeds 1 and 2. Run both directions for each condition, plus matching static-square and blank controls. Include one vertical and one horizontal bright-square diagnostic, but moving bright squares contain both ON and OFF edge events and therefore do not isolate the T4 ON pathway.

Fit only a small **offline diagnostic** nearest-centroid decoder on the eight calibration trials: four features are local T5a/b/c/d spike counts divided by corresponding local cell counts; standardize using calibration mean and standard deviation, floored at .05; one centroid per direction. Score the 12 held-out OFF trials, retaining their position/speed labels. Also apply the bar-trained decoder to the same square trials to distinguish shape-transfer from within-shape decodability. Neither decoder is an in-network learned readout or a production component. Report whether a single threshold on total normalized T5 activity can separate moving from static squares, and whether raw T4 subtype spikes carry any moving signal. A meaningful front-end result is at least 10/12 four-way held-out accuracy with no static/blank confusion; otherwise identify the failing directions and move to a different motion-front-end hypothesis rather than a narrow gain sweep.
