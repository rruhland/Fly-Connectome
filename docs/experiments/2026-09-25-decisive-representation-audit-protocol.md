# Decisive M1A latent representation audit

**Scope:** One frozen-model, paired-input representation audit. No production change, no online direction supervision, no Pong semantics, and no follow-up parameter sweep. The learned encoder/recurrent state is the same event-centric model used in the sparse-coherence forecasts, trained on 64 generic fractional-motion episodes. Its weights are frozen before every probe scene. The measured-graph comparison uses frozen T4/T5 spike responses from the same event-camera images under the previously approved Tm4 supplement and T5 order-current dynamics; it does not modify connectome weights.

**Probe:** An evaluation-only nearest-centroid direction decoder receives normalized local activity histograms. Its four direction centroids are fitted once from single dark/bright squares at two positions and one-pixel/frame speed. The same centroids score held-out positions, two-pixel/frame speed, held-out plus shape, two separated movers, and crossing movers. The probe is run on (1) fixed event-correlation primitives, (2) directly observed learned units, (3) the learned recurrent hidden state, (4) frozen T4/T5 subtype spikes, and (5) hidden state plus T4/T5. It never sees a future frame during feature extraction. Object masks and direction labels are available only to the offline probe and scoring, yielding an upper bound on representation quality rather than a deployable object tracker. Report activity coverage and accuracy by direction, polarity, and domain.

**Decision rule:** Preserve the current latent and redesign prediction only if its frozen recurrent state decodes at least 80% on both held-out position and speed, at least 75% on separated multi-entity cases, and materially beats a randomized-label centroid control. Crossing is an additional stress result, not a mandatory pass. Otherwise abandon the per-site/lag-stack recurrent latent family for M1A. If T4/T5 adds a large gain, treat those measured-graph responses as evidence for a new front end, not as a retroactive pass for the current latent. The fixed correlation control checks whether the generated scenes contain decodable motion. No threshold, window, classifier, or state parameter will be tuned after seeing the held-out results.
