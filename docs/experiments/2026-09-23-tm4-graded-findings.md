# Tm4 current release recovers the fast arm, but misses the static-specificity gate

The [preregistered opt-in probe](2026-09-23-tm4-graded-protocol.md) added a spike-silent, current-driven Tm4 supplement on **6,572 existing Tm4→T5 feedforward edges** (52,647 measured contacts, all with their recorded positive sign and one-tick delay). It kept ordinary Tm4 spikes, the selected Tm9 release, fixed T5 order current, long-term weights, and the pinned checkpoint unchanged. Blank-only calibration selected cap 0.10: added T5 impulse p99 was 0.005 and there were no blank T5 spikes during cap selection. See the [machine-readable result](2026-09-23-tm4-graded-results.json).

At the formerly weak y=16, two-pixel/frame **T5d/up** condition, Tm4 arm support on the comparator's same future-gate event samples rose from **48.7% to 95.2%** (+46.5 percentage points). Direction-contrast signs remained T5c-up/T5d-down at both calibration positions and both held-out speeds. Absolute contrasts grew at every case: T5c/T5d were −55/+59 → −78/+69 at y=10, −96/+41 → −106/+48 at y=22, −73/+49 → −113/+49 at y=16/speed 1, and −22/+20 → −65/+25 at y=16/speed 2. This is strong evidence that a source-local subthreshold signal can traverse the measured Tm4 contacts and rescue the missing motion arm without destroying direction tuning in this frozen test.

The full feature gate nevertheless **fails** its fixed static-specificity requirement. For T5c at y=16/speed 1, preferred-moving spikes rose 243→253, but static-bar spikes rose 56→65; the moving/static ratio fell 4.339→3.892, retaining **89.7%** of baseline against the required 90%. Other cases met the ratio and blank thresholds, but the static increase is systematic for T5c (63→69 at y=10, 67→70 at y=22, and 56→65 at y=16/speed 2). Do not round this into a pass or promote the profile. The result isolates a much narrower bottleneck than missing information: Tm4's usable current is present and transmissible, while the simple release law also strengthens static responses. A next opt-in experiment should preregister a source-local temporal-selectivity criterion or independently chosen release dynamics, then test it on new motion/static controls before attempting afferent plasticity. No production architecture or learning rule changed.
