# M1A.5 motion-cadence memory: bounded candidate rejected

The locally learned cadence table is **not** an M1A state or M1A.5 dynamics model to keep. It was trained from 48 visible event sequences with no hidden locations, object labels, backpropagation or reward. It learned 168 local consecutive-speed associations across eight occupied transition bins. The tracklet scaffold supplied causal moving components; the learned table only changed autonomous motion while evidence was absent. Production M1A is unchanged.

The predeclared [gate](../plans/2026-09-26-m1a5-history-state-benchmark.md) required at least 14/16 late-hidden changing-motion top-8 and top-32 hits, all 16/16 constant-motion top-32 hits, and at least 14/16 two-entity top-64 hits. In event-only inference, the learned model scored **12/16 top-8, 16/16 top-32** on changing motion, **8/16 top-32** on constant motion, and **12/16 top-64** on two movers. The same tracklet scaffold with its transition table frozen scored **12/16, 16/16; 16/16; 16/16** respectively. Sparse-frame inference produced identical counts. Thus learning supplied no top-8 improvement, did not beat its stronger frozen control at top-32, and damaged transfer to constant motion. The candidate fails all three jointly required checks.

The stronger frozen scaffold also reveals a protocol defect: even the changing-motion split was saturated at top-32 before learning. The earlier single-target tracker's 12/16 was not a sufficiently strong control. This experiment was run once under the registered gate; its failure is not a reason to tune thresholds or keep revising the same cadence table. The decisive next benchmark must include a *visible contextual regularity* that predicts a change behind missing evidence and varies independently of object shape, position and speed. A generic fixed kinematic tracker should then be below a learnable ceiling, while a shuffled-context learner stays at the tracker baseline. Include false persistence and correction after contradictory reveal. This would test learned world dynamics, rather than a slight improvement in extrapolation of already decodable velocity.

The observer's original exact rendered-intensity training teacher is still privileged. Event-only and hybrid in this report describe **inference input**, not teacher-free observer training. Reproduce with `.venv/Scripts/python scripts/run_local_cadence_memory.py`; [raw results](2026-09-26-local-cadence-memory-results.json) include both sensor arms and the learned/frozen comparison.
