## Why revisit parameter search

Earlier eight-direction linear-head search, action-dose adjustments and larger
practice banks showed memorization and poor transfer. Those failures matter.
The new test differs in the now-approved nonlinear512feature output space and
exact preservation of earned early behavior and a previously acquired later skill.
It tests whether complete-game reward can choose compatible improvements without
requiring imitation of a selected donor trajectory. It is not a novel search method
or evidence that random search will work here.

Parent revisited [Mania, Guy and Recht2018](https://papers.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2018/hash/7634ea65a4e6d9041cfd3f7de18e334a-Abstract.html).
Their parameter-search results motivate a cheap baseline; their variability warning
also matters. Our constrained discrete-action search is not their ARS algorithm,
and their continuous-control results do not establish visual Pong performance.

Final numeric conditioning, calibration and selection rules must be persisted
before fitting or candidate game evaluation. Reused Sol implements and challenges;
parent reviews gates and actual outcomes. Bridge work remains another thread.
