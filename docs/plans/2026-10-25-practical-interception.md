## Alternatives being challenged while block2 runs

The legal512row buffer spans180directions at a relative1e−8threshold, but90%of its second-moment energy lies in25modes (99%in71). This is evidence of uneven conditioning on the observed states, not a global capacity ceiling. Ridge-conditioned exploration within the existing transform is a candidate if needed. Higher spatial modes would expand the optimizer's restriction within the already-existing linear head; they may also worsen sample efficiency or memorization, so dimension expansion is not the automatic next step.

A more substantive alternative is a reward-earned action-regression proposal: use the learner's own successful exploratory trajectories, restricted to inputs where that child changed the incumbent's action and the paired child hit while the incumbent missed. Fit a regularized residual with anchor-success preservation, control induced action changes, and test the resulting policy in actual closed-loop games. No privileged teacher actions or backpropagation. This is not yet implemented or selected. Credit assignment, compensated mistakes and distribution shift remain real concerns.

Related sources: [Peters and Schaal, ICML2007](https://www.ias.informatik.tu-darmstadt.de/uploads/Publications/Publications/ICML2007-Peters_4493%5B0%5D.pdf) derives reward-weighted regression for immediate-reward robot control; Pong's delayed rewards violate that setting. [Peng et al., AWR2019](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.00177) describes value regression and weighted action likelihood fitting. Our proposed closed-form linear residual would be an adaptation motivated by these ideas, not their exact algorithms or a transfer of their guarantees.
