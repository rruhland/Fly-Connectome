# Proposed M1A visual-architecture amendment

**Status: proposed, not implemented.** This would change the [original Milestone 1 design](2026-09-16-milestone-1-design.md), which explicitly limits M1 to spiking point neurons, defers graded potentials and dendritic compartments, and excludes LC11. The current production checkpoint, graph, and learning loop remain unchanged until this amendment is approved and an opt-in prototype passes the gates below.

## Why the original architecture is now the bottleneck

The original M1A acceptance test requires online next-event learning better than persistence and direction/velocity-related anticipatory activity. The frozen measured graph has active lamina cells but no robust T4/T5 direction stage ([motion-stage audit](../experiments/2026-09-22-motion-stage-audit-findings.md)). T3-only rest current and simple T4 input-arm combinations did not rescue it ([T3 test](../experiments/2026-09-23-t3-small-target-findings.md), [T4 test](../experiments/2026-09-23-t4-compartment-input-findings.md)).

A second measured route is directly relevant to the Pong ball: all 75 traced right LC11 cells receive T2, T2a, and T3 input, and 87.3% of their incoming contact mass comes from neurons already in the M1 roster ([wiring audit](../experiments/2026-09-23-lc11-wiring-findings.md)). The present network's T2/T2a/T3 cells do not spike under small-dot probes, yet T2 membrane/current state carries a reproducible local ON/OFF signal at x=18 and x=46 ([subthreshold test](../experiments/2026-09-23-object-pathway-subthreshold-findings.md)). This signal is lost at the spike-only output boundary.

Simply adding LC11 is insufficient. Fixed and 250 ms adaptive voltage-release laws on the existing T2/T3→LC11 contacts mainly increased blank firing, with no reliable ON excess ([fixed](../experiments/2026-09-23-lc11-shadow-findings.md), [adaptive](../experiments/2026-09-23-lc11-adaptive-findings.md)). Adding all measured signed LC11 inputs scarcely changed the result because major inhibitory source classes were spike-silent ([signed-input test](../experiments/2026-09-23-lc11-signed-input-findings.md)). Li15 and MeLo10 were also flat below threshold, while their dominant measured afferents are respectively T2 and T2a—both spike-silent ([inhibitory voltage audit](../experiments/2026-09-23-lc11-inhibitory-voltage-findings.md)). The failure propagates through the optic-lobe graph. A new scalar gain, rest current, or prediction target alone is unlikely to fix it.

## Recommended scope change

Bring **cell-class-specific graded visual transmission** into M1A as a hybrid with the existing spiking network. A visual source may express a bounded local presynaptic release state in addition to spikes; release travels only along that source's **existing measured edges** with the recorded sign and delay. It must be driven by causal state available at that cell, not by an event label, future observation, Pong state, or a global decoder. Keep measured contact weights as the initial magnitudes and maintain local eligibility/plasticity on each existing synapse. The central and DNa02 pathways remain spiking. The original spike-only checkpoint remains the frozen comparator.

This amendment would also permit a **retinotopically local postsynaptic integration state** where evidence requires it. Any grouping must be derived from measured afferent identities and annotated/inferred columns; no synthetic connection, shared trainable convolution kernel, or direction label may be introduced. An LC11-inclusive roster is *optional* and separately gated. The first priority remains recovering T4/T5 motion responses in the existing M1 roster, because fly physiology places direction selectivity there; LC11 is an object-presence branch rather than a substitute motion stage.

The exact release and local integration equations should be chosen by bounded opt-in tests, not hardcoded from the failed LC11 shadow laws. The next prototype should specifically test propagation of the **already observed** T2/T2a/T3 voltage signal through all their measured downstream edges, including Li15/MeLo10, against no-event blank and bar/static controls. It must measure whether the inhibitory branch becomes responsive without autonomous saturation before adding LC11 to production. In parallel, the existing T4/T5 paths need class-specific impulse-response validation of Mi4/Tm4/Tm9 and correct ON/OFF E/I timing. These are design-validation experiments; they do not themselves alter the production architecture.

## Promotion gates

1. **Transmission:** Across x=18 and x=46, both ON and OFF local dot/edge responses propagate through measured pathways with low blank activity, finite state, and no wrong-sign transmitter current. Report source activity, E/I arrivals, and per-cell distribution, not only a pooled score.
2. **Feature expression:** Frozen T4/T5 responses show a consistent subtype-specific motion preference at both locations and a speed holdout, with static/bar controls. Any optional LC11 branch detects a small moving object at both locations with low quiet-frame false alarms; characterize static and bar responses rather than assuming they must be zero. Neither stage may pass solely through tonic firing.
3. **Local online learning:** On the controlled repeated-motion task, plasticity improves held-out next-event anticipation over its own frozen checkpoint and persistence at a withheld position/tempo. Updates remain `local prediction error × local eligibility`, with no backprop, global trained readout, or synthetic error network.
4. **M1A integration:** Repeat the pinned Pong open-loop evaluation. Require decreasing local prediction error, better-than-persistence held-out prediction, direction/velocity anticipation, and stable sparse firing before M1B. Preserve the original spike-only run as a comparator and benchmark the new dynamics against the training-speed target.

If the opt-in transmission gate fails, do **not** promote the hybrid model. Reassess sensory transduction and target-cell physiology with primary fly data before changing the production graph. If it passes, implement the smallest in-place hybrid profile required, version its checkpoint/model schema, and run CPU/reference parity and full regression tests after each logical chunk. This is an explicit design amendment, not a silent reinterpretation of the original M1 architecture.
