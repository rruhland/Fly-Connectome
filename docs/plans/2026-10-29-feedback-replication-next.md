# Next acquisition and sustained-play checks

The relational scaffold has produced fresh improvement: 90 to 131 returns,
with later-return success rising from 71.4% to 89.4%. The remaining task is
dependability, not another broad controller search. The current deployment
comparison tests two concrete mechanisms: using the learned controller during
the opening, and preserving its selected public visual identities through
observation gaps. Freeze the retained procedure after those comparisons.

The proposed next acquisition test uses two entirely new, disjoint, balanced
16-start TRAIN cohorts. Each replica starts from the carried 40069 policy and
evaluates the same 16 generic zero-numeric structural candidates. No previous
winning binding gets preference. Stable nested cohorts of 4, 8 and 16 starts
retain 4 then 2 candidates; only complete 16-start reward totals can replace the
incumbent, with baseline retained on ties. Pre-draw one uniform structural initial
control per replica and finish its 16 games as an additional learning reference.
If that control is already competent, report it honestly.

Budget each acquisition at 240 seconds and 128,000 transition cameras, including
new baseline games and control completion. This is at most 124 standard games.
Vary the experience cohorts and proposal/tie order. Identical recovered bindings
are a desirable replication result; the meaningful independence is new experience,
not merely a different tie seed. No numeric coefficient sweep is currently justified.

Freeze both endpoints before evaluating either on new games. Proposed validation
per replica: a predetermined balanced 32 games lasting up to 60 seconds and a
separate balanced 32 lasting up to 20 seconds. Fix all starts and duration
assignments before acquisition. Require approximately 95% first and later return
success, with at least 200 later opportunities in each long-game cohort. Report
duration strata and actual exposure in the 0–20, 20–40 and 40–60 second intervals;
do not hide late failures by pooling early successes. Insufficient exposure is
incomplete evidence, not permission for a success-dependent extension.

Candidate-only validation has a worst case of 256,000 transition cameras and
128 initial observations across both replicas, roughly 14 minutes at recent
audited throughput. Running matched baseline games for every fresh start doubles
that cost. Acquisition already includes matched baseline controls, and the prior
fresh comparison establishes a substantial improvement, so absolute reliability
is the priority for this validation. The final execution specification must pin
sources, schedules, duration support and a sufficient wall budget before launch.

This plan is conditional on the ongoing deployment comparison; it launches no
work by itself. Parent selected the practical replication question and new
experience requirement; reused Sol `learning_challenge` developed and challenged
the reduced acquisition and duration-stratified validation design. Success would
establish a reproducible Pong feedback scaffold over shared frozen vision. It
would not establish general game learning, continuous integration of all stages,
or completion of the broader M1B/connectome milestone.
