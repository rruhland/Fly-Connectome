# Integration hypotheses and decision boundaries

Written while the minimum-phase run remained blind. This is a research decision aid, not authorization for another allocation in the bounded session.

| Explanation | Evidence so far | What would distinguish it | What would not establish it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mean objective permits phase sacrifice | Balanced run learned LEFT and damaged RIGHT despite equal practice | Current minimum-phase training, with unchanged inputs/search/sampling and fresh heldout retention | A single new-seed success does not isolate objective causality |
| Small training banks produce unreliable proposals | Six starts/phase; conditional resampling changes update directions substantially | Independent fresh TRAIN confirmation of proposed versus current deployed head; or separately designed matched coverage study | Resampling one observed bank does not estimate the true learning gradient or reproduce independent training |
| Combined update differs from tested candidates | Perturbed heads are scored, then a weighted update creates an untested center | Fresh center/proposal confirmation before acceptance, with unchanged final DEV | Better candidate fitness alone does not show the deployed center improved |
| Initialization/path dependence | Both phases learn individually; RIGHT and DIRECT are different learned heads | Same fresh banks/directions/objective/budget from both starts, compare absolute endpoints and retention | One uncontrolled DIRECT restart confounds initialization with fresh-context luck |
| Representation or hidden-state limitation | Integration failures, but both phases acquire with same frozen visual/linear class | Constructive success weakens incompatibility; a reviewed zero-effect legal history extension with matched control tests added information | Failed interpolation, differing actions on diverged trajectories, or one nonlinear-feature null do not prove old-class impossibility |

The current visual encoder already binds signed observed velocity to16x16 position bins and relative motion to9x9 relation bins; four-neighbor relations and visual forecasts are present. The fixed214-coordinate projection and linear greedy decision boundaries may still limit use of this information. Action history is not explicit, but observed paddle velocity is available: missing motor-state information remains a hypothesis, not a diagnosis. Major extensions require user review.

Astra raised candidate-to-center mismatch after reading Mania, Guy and Recht, [section2.1 and Algorithm2](https://arxiv.org/html/1803.07055v1): basic random search estimates a smoothed objective and takes an update without line search. Our normalized directions, top4 selection and binary phase objective do not inherit the simple Gaussian estimator's unbiasedness claim. Successful original-scale acquisition and failed earlier small-dose trials argue against an automatic noise reduction. Sol independently favored fresh proposal confirmation over another scale sweep if minimum-phase updates remain substantive but trade skills.

One possible future confirmation design—not selected or staged—is16fresh training starts per phase shared by current/proposed heads,64additional episodes per population versus192candidate episodes. Accept under a prospectively fixed balanced-gain/per-phase criterion; on rejection preserve weights but advance proposal RNG. This costs about one-third more episodes per proposal and reduces attainable updates. Finite-bank false acceptances/rejections remain possible; confirmation plus acceptance is a bundled mechanism intervention, not pure proof of smoothing error. No heldout games select weights. Specify criterion/power and complete budgets before any implementation.

The population method is fresh-data episodic reward learning. What remains unproven is a single declared procedure that acquires, retains and progresses through all stages without our manual scaffold changes—not a requirement that every parameter update occur within a game. Reliable first interception, repeated rallying, placement and cross-game learning remain separate evidence targets.

For the current run: joint learning/retention success calls for independent recipe validation; learning with harm remains an integration tradeoff; negligible updates/partial execution permit only a dose-limited conclusion. A substantial moving null calls for a strategic mechanism reassessment. Stop this session after checking the outcome; do not run a finer mixture grid, arbitrary restart or parameter sweep.
