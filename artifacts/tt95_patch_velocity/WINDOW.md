# TT95 · Hold the motion through the moment of impact

The decisive change in these two exact mounted hard-ball diagnostics was to fit a short **window of motion**, not only the best velocity vector at one instant. Fitting the same attained patch at a single precontact clock recovered broad contact but produced no legal return. Tracking four control boundaries through contact recovered two already-attained shots. Neither diagnostic is an RGB-only result.

| Native inverse condition | 14429 | 7398 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Single precontact snapshot, projected-normal velocity | Miss, 23.4 mm | Miss, 48.4 mm |
| Single precontact snapshot, full velocity | Miss, 26.5 mm | Miss, 53.7 mm |

| Motion window, locally corrected full-velocity inverse | Legal and answered, 33.2 mm | Legal and answered, 67.7 mm |

The mounted body was commissioned independently of this study, and neither release trained the learned inverse or the public receiving model. The unchanged reference trajectories were previously proved on the same hardware. Only in this evaluator-controlled diagnostic do those old successes define the phase clock and target patch motion. The motor fit is recomputed from attained starting encoders and issued references, without access to the successful control suffix. The locally corrected condition receives a body-only dynamics check with no ball or opponent. The real native prefix, far contact, near bounce and physical referee are held fixed, and every motion-window legal answer reproduced its exact event digest. All tested motions had zero sampled CAD penetration.

The sharpest contrast was 14429. The 25.5 mm full learned window reached the face but sent the ball forward at 2.54 m/s and failed. The 27.5 mm projected-normal window sent it at 3.08 m/s and earned a reply. A body-only correction to the full window also made a real return at 3.17 m/s. This separates geometric contact, useful signed impulse and awareness of trajectory phase. Adding tangent is neither necessary nor sufficient on these cases. The two 7398 answers were near the rim and do not demonstrate a new broad success.
