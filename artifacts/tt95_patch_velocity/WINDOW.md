The motor fit is recomputed from attained starting encoders and issued references, without access to the successful control suffix. The body-screened condition tests potential changes with a body-only dynamics check with no ball or opponent. Its initial late-clock nominal command survived screening unchanged in both cases. The no-ball patch models were not used as proof of the score. The real native prefix, far contact, near bounce and physical referee are held fixed, and every motion-window legal answer reproduced its exact event digest. All tested motions had zero sampled CAD penetration.

The sharpest contrast was 14429. The 25.5 mm full learned window reached the face but sent the ball forward at 2.54 m/s and failed. The 27.5 mm projected-normal window sent it at 3.08 m/s and earned a reply. The late-clock initial full program retained by a native body screen also made a real return at 3.17 m/s. This separates geometric contact, useful signed impulse and awareness of trajectory phase. Adding tangent is neither necessary nor sufficient on these cases. The two 7398 answers were near the rim and do not demonstrate a new broad success.

## Public forecasts on two matched native low shots

A separate actor derived its contact clock from ordinary RGB and the velocities/normals from scored encoder history rather than the original successful motion. Terminal and contact-window policies each issued a novel candidate that was nominally legal in the public twin on both 14429 and 15541. All four actual matched balls still failed.
