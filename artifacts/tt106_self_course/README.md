## Teaching and policy

The student rolls from a standard measured launch. Before the near bounce, the trainer snapshots the actual reached body, existing reference, independent opponent sequence, and current public stereo history. The student finishes its natural attempt without the coach. Actual good movements provide positive labels, while the student's actual misses provide negative support. In a separate practice branch only, the teacher searches whole reference trajectories from the captured state. Independent cubic preparation and strike parameters and an optional correction of the just-attempted movement vary what was done long before the last 0.2 seconds. A native rollout can shortlist but cannot pay a lesson. A matching physical receive, legal landing, ordinary answer, clear samples and exact replay are needed for reward.

The pupil learns a local linear map from camera phase, joint position, encoder differences and already-issued reference to the next signed joint rates. It does not replay the teacher's action tape, or receive teacher state or a future bounce in a game. Sufficiently supported low preparation is blended early. When the standard actor has no accepted legal solution, the same recurrent per-tick policy can generate a full alternative for the ordinary public uncertainty check. The normal controller's accepted plan is protected.

This is a staged data-aggregation experiment, not expert control of the assessed point. Later training fades in more student control to visit the states it actually induces. The first 14-point policy did not alter any practice actions at its conservative gate. Later practice uses a lower gate to collect such states and stays in points longer to expose actual terminal errors. Official evaluation keeps the original stronger safety condition and uses a separate release roster. The pilot is not fed back to the student.
