# Online three-action learner after Pong

The question is whether native Pong experience accelerates an arm given only three body-relative lateral actions. All learners share stereo features, the local jog size, the arm’s practiced swing, and the same physical coaching and continuation. The action alphabet is left, center and right regardless of source history. The chooser has no supplied mapping between visual position and the desired action. Its transferable preference was estimated from paired native durations. The ordinal method was motivated by earlier movement magnitudes failing to transfer. On a previous physical opening bank, 5/5 native fits chose profitable directions, but one pooled shuffle made the exact same choices. The previously reserved fixed-policy comparison therefore closed. This experiment asks a new question about open-ended choice and target feedback on independent complete points. [Source study](direction.md) · [Prior fixed-policy gate](direction-design.md)

Use four fixed eight-point practice streams. The three physical alternatives for every start share the same camera history until their first lateral choice. A learner sees only the elapsed duration of the full point it actually chose. Its body resets for the next point and its experience carries. All alternatives must reproduce by strict physical replay. The bank allows a reproducible comparison across prospective exploration choices; the extra alternatives are unavailable to the learner. The launch is the independent physical unit. Use two prior observations per action, with means .75, .50 and .25 for the favored, central and opposed actions given the current camera observation. The flat prior assigns .50 to all three. A tied source preference also gives all three .50. At deployment both apply their highest prior on the first point; a tie holds the center. Every later decision uses the same paired beta exploration draws. Update only the selected action with elapsed time capped at ten seconds. Generalize previous action outcomes to the current visual context with the existing Gaussian kernel. Mirrored contexts and actions share credit equally; both learners use the same visual representations and symmetry. The flat learner has no source-derived motor option or reward data. Keep fixed center, frozen native, the preregistered shuffled native fit, and reversed directional preference visible. Run 256 paired exploration sequences per physical stream. Simulation exposure in seconds to first ten is meaningful only when success occurs; both point censoring and interaction time are reported. Do not treat exploration draws as new environments.

Stop after eight points per learner; photographically and physically inspect any witness as needed. This is a check of the online entry point.
