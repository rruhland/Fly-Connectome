# Learning which visual transitions predict continued play

Fit on archived complete native Pong episodes and use the archive's original 18 training and 12 held launch seeds. Group each seed's six policies. Every fourth optical frame supplies a seven-frame window of actual observations. Read mover and action-identified paddle positions at the first, middle and last frames. Use magnitudes and normalized dot products of mover displacement, actor displacement and mover-relative-to-actor position. Measure displacement in court-width units. Fit the same ridge basis in both games. The model receives observed positions only. Its features omit hit flags, time to impact, forecast trajectories and elapsed time. Train on `1 - exp(-seconds remaining / 2)` after estimating the effect of elapsed time with a clock-only spline. Shuffle the residual duration labels in 15-frame elapsed-time bins for the matched null. Give each original Pong seed equal weight in the loss. Source held tests use mean per-seed error and paired policy order at common seed and time.

Apply the frozen visual value at frame 24 in each verified SO-101 jog-bank point. The seven-frame window spans frames 18 through 24. Fit nothing to the arm outcomes. Use actual stereo mover observations and rebuild learner paddle motion by applying issued joint rates to the independent factory twin. A start is eligible when every distinct physical option supplies all three sightings. Collapse identical full command tapes. The visual value is available during the point, at a fixed time shared by all options. For the clock control, all options on a start tie. Frame 30 is an explicitly secondary observation with separately reported coverage.

Evaluate paired ordering for strict learner legal returns, strictly alternating rally length, and reaching ten alternations. Give each start equal weight for an endpoint and score an equal predicted value as half a success. Require lower source held mean absolute error than both controls, at least .55 source held pair concordance with at least .05 improvement over shuffled labels, and at least ten verified arm starts with differing learner returns and complete three-sighting windows. Require arm legal-return concordance of at least .65 and a .10 advantage over shuffled labels at frame 24. Meet all gates before considering further physical trials or an online choice learner. Report the scope of target coverage and the prevalence of held features near the source training data. Neither feature selection nor a gate depends on physical target outcomes.
