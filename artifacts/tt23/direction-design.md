# Transferring an ordinal motor direction

This protocol was chosen after the seven-action Pong and arm experiments showed that different native training sets tended to choose different movement magnitudes. Separate the direction decision from the body-specific step. At each native launch compare the actual terminal duration under the positive and negative action of each of the three equal magnitudes. A longer positive action earns one, a longer negative action earns minus one, and a tie earns zero. Average the three votes equally. Fit the same reflected Gaussian support rule to the signed votes using the existing anonymous mover features and motor axis. Reflection flips the two lateral features and the vote. The model supplies a preference for one direction. A missing scene or tied preference holds the center.

Use every recorded seven-option launch in the original 72-start source and four independent 48-start sources. Train each independently and train the pooled fit. Freeze one within-scene duration shuffle for each source, before computing the signed votes. Retain the same features and kernel parameters under both labels. Compare the pooled learned, centered, shuffled and sign-reversed selections. The reference arm pose and the smallest symmetric nonzero step come from each body's existing motor menu. The arm step is .07632 m. No interception location enters the direction decision.

The genuine native holdout contains 48 new launches from offset 1284500000. Physically play the three smallest choices at each start and compare the four frozen policies by selecting only their respective observed option from the same-prefix, scored tapes. Evaluate the five independent fits and their direction agreement on these held native views. The same frozen policies then select from the completed seven-action physical arm bank of 64 new launches from generator 846093157. The arm bank was coordinated before its outcomes and is generated independently of the model. Compare strict first legal returns, contacts, and paired gains and losses. Advance to eight new full receiving starts only if the pooled learner lengthens native points relative to center and shuffle, at least four of the five individual native fits agree with the pooled direction on at least 75% of detected held native views, and the pooled arm fit earns more legal returns than both center and shuffle. Retain the trained opening correction, physical learned partner, and the common tick-25 continuation. Compare frozen pooled, centered and shuffled choices on all eight starts from generator 795923117. Score actual alternating lengths, legal returns, wins, and 10- and 20-return counts. The 64-arm opening comparison uses the existing shared stock motor stroke, while the gated full points use the previously trained correction in every branch.
