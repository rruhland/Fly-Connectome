# Live, independently varied opponent on the regulation court

The TT37 far paddle sustained 200 consecutive legal returns with an independent broad placement draw on every reception, when paired with a second, privileged physical carriage. The unchanged camera/encoder SO-101 pupil reached at most seven alternating returns under the same far protocol. The far paddle legally answered every one of that pupil's 19 legal returns. The 200-return result tests the opponent's capacity, not learned or articulated SO-101 performance. [Uninterrupted 200-return tape](opponent-capacity-witness.json.gz) · [Frozen pupil, random opponent](opponent-baseline-random-long.json.gz)

## What is random and what is physical

The default opponent draws four coordinates independently for each live reception. Lateral aim is uniform from −0.28 to +0.28 m, including the center, and the other uniforms are −1.34 to −1.22 m depth, 1.01 to 1.11 m height, and 3.15 to 3.98 m/s pace toward the near player. A draw is a placement goal, not a ball impulse or a forced landing. The current ball state and the finite-mass carriage pose determine whether and how the far paddle can physically hit toward it. Replans retain the same draw. A net-cord opportunity may use a shorter stroke with the same requested placement. The optional narrow `lane` profile exists as an explicit control and was not used in the broad results below. [Arena and explicit distributions](../../scripts/experimental/tt37_opponent.py)

The recorded ball state is installed once at the opening. Thereafter the native solver advances the ball, both paddles, the table and the net, and only a paddle collision followed by the legal bounce earns a return. The far carriage is force-driven and capped rather than repositioned to catch the ball. All later draw receipts were checked against the independent seeded draw and their own preceding physical learner contact. The simulator checks time advances on every control exchange and the replay checks the final solver time against the action count. It stops only at the physical referee decision, the declared return cap, or the time limit. The long cap is 200 alternating legal returns and the diagnostic cap is 24. [Seed and release manifest](opponent-plan.json)

The manifest separates 18 practice contexts, 24 archive-disjoint new measured first releases, and four familiar first releases with four independently seeded continuations each. The 24 new IDs were selected without testing either player's outcomes or screening for actual deliveries. The 16 continuation cards anchor the old first placement to expose differences on later shots. The raw learner receives camera images and encoders, with no target, seed or protocol input. All 16 anchored first balls matched exactly across protocols. Both protocols delivered 18 of the 24 new first releases. The same six aborted in the first five control ticks, before the opponent could play, on a fixture collision or double hit. Different random goal realizations on the remaining fresh openings are part of the protocol comparison, so the aggregate scores are not a matched test of a change to the pupil. On anchored cards the pupil landed 14 and 18 shots under the new and old later streams, respectively. TT37 measured 29 left, 10 middle and 14 right crossings of the near −1.10 m receive line, with a 24.7 mm median gap between requested and actual x there. Unusual legal pupil shots meant a landing beyond 0.30 m from center or within 0.50 m of the net, or a ball departure speed outside 2.8–6.0 m/s. TT37 answered 12/12 on these and TT36 answered 22/22. ## Intrinsic far-paddle capacity

The feasibility control replaces the near arm's paddle with a separate mirrored finite-mass carriage. It is not a substitute for the learning task. It lets a stable physical near striker produce a stream of legal outgoing balls without scripted trajectories, restarts, or simulated catches. On release 5530 with reply seed 374301 the broad TT37 opponent and this counterpart each landed 100 shots over 155.38 simulated seconds. The cap stopped after the 100th near return, so there were 99 eligible later opponent replies and it delivered all 99. Its actual receive-line x locations covered −0.260 to +0.264 m, with 36 left, 33 middle and 31 right. Both L→R and R→L switches occurred (12 and 13). The same lateral bands also repeated (eight cases per band), inconsistent with an alternating policy. Median x target error there was 6.5 mm. [200-return witness](opponent-capacity-witness.json.gz)

The witness replay matched every referee event and identified all 200 hits as active native ball/blade solver contacts from their assigned physical carriages. It checked all 74,584 integration states. None of the tested moving-carriage pairs came within the 25 mm clearance horizon of the table or net. [Long-point contact and clearance proof](opponent-capacity-audit.json)

All four predeclared stable-near capacity starts, 5530, 14832, 3025 and 8239, reached 200 without a point stop, each on its own continuation seed. That gave 396/396 further legal opponent replies after eligible near returns across the four points. Only the first point was independently replayed with the continuous CAD audit. [Complete four-start capacity check](opponent-capacity-central-random.json.gz)

A second diagnostic deliberately changed the near carriage's desired outgoing side, depth, height and pace independently to stress the far responder. Four physical games each reached the 24-return diagnostic cap. Among their 44 eligible legal near returns, 30 met the unusual-shot threshold above. The far opponent answered all 44, with near-side strike departure speeds as high as 6.99 m/s and actual far-table bounce x up to 0.353 m from center. None of these measured trials established a miss rate for more extreme shots. [Four wider-reply stress tapes](opponent-capacity-wide-random.json.gz)

## Reproduce

Use the pinned measured feed archive and EGL. Run `python -m scripts.experimental.tt37_opponent_benchmark plan /tmp/tt25-rallies.json`, then `baseline /tmp/tt25-rallies.json --long --protocol random` and `baseline /tmp/tt25-rallies.json --long --protocol tt36`, followed by `summary /tmp/tt25-rallies.json` in the same module. Use `python -m scripts.experimental.tt37_opponent_capacity /tmp/tt25-rallies.json --witness` for the standalone long point and `--near wide --cap 24` for the stress set. Run `python -m scripts.experimental.tt37_opponent_audit`, optionally with `--capacity artifacts/tt37/opponent-capacity-witness.json.gz`, for the full geometry/contacts replay.
