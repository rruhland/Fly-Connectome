# Replace SO-101 serving practice with factory-model proposals

A server chosen entirely in a separate factory-model simulation won both new native RGB games 11–7 with the existing rollout receiver. It served legally on 15 of 16 own attempts. This replaced the previous server’s 1,004 target practice points and 38,526 control frames on the tested compact station. Two 12-attempt correction procedures selected the same different candidate. That candidate lost one paired game 9–11 and won the other 11–8. The server starts with canonical joint coordinates from the published SO-101 geometry and the nominal gripped paddle. It proposes tool paths around a legal descending toss and converts each to a timed motor sweep. The independent simulator uses the same nominal plant, actuator increments, compact court and contact parameters as the native target fixture. Its ball launches come from independent seeds and a wider distribution around public serving geometry. Every launch starts behind the end line. The sampled positions span −0.070 to +0.072 m laterally, a −0.929 to −0.863 m longitudinal design range, a 0.316 to 0.383 m height range above the table, and a 2.67 to 3.035 m/s upward speed range. It uses no target stroke bank or target trajectory for proposal generation. Its collision paths supplied an explicit score for own bounce, net clearance and the far bounce, with opponent-racket contact disabled. The server search took 432 virtual tapes of 51 control frames each, or 22,032 factory-model frames, and approximately 5.8 seconds on the host. Each shortlisted motion made five nominally legal serves across the eight synthetic scenarios. The factory-only branch froze the highest ranked proposal before any target practice. Native play used wrist and fixed stereo RGB to choose lateral aim. The receiving branch retained the same independent dry factory catalogue, rollout ranker, and gantry flight and impact priors across the four games. The factory rollout costs and the previously learned gantry priors are still part of this configuration. The comparison is specific to a station with an accurate nominal factory model and a known court. [Dry catalogue](factory-dry-catalog.npz) · [Gantry ball flight prior](flight-source.json.gz)

The repeated procedure compared four candidates on the same three toss seeds. The chronological procedure presented each candidate once per round in three independently shuffled rounds and drew a new target toss seed for every attempt. Correction used scalar point reward and RGB placement across the net. The referee’s legal-serve labels were archived for evaluation. The two procedures represent 24 research attempts in total, with a 12-attempt budget per fitted selector. Both selected the same fixed proposal, so the chronological branch reuses that proposal’s held games. Rebinding its separate checkpoint to the published optical tape reproduced the exact commands and subsequent rollout plan.
