# Carrying movements from earlier games

The starting controller retained 768 movements. The next 24 games earned 787 more. One consolidation rule added 192 observations whose motor response recurred in several independent points. Its comparison added 192 observations sampled at similar levels of novelty without requiring recurrence.

| Frozen motor bank | Returns on 24 assigned points | Median | Peak |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Before further practice | 189 | 6 | 26 |
| Ordinary moving 768-fragment bank after practice | 135 | 3.5 | 25 |
| Starting bank plus 192 recurring examples | 184 | 5 | 26 |
| Starting bank plus 192 novelty-matched sample examples | 217 | 6 | 29 |

Keeping a sample of the new experience helped on this assignment. Requiring multiple episodes to confirm a movement did not improve on sampling it. The next live comparison retains all newly earned movements inside a point, then stores a bounded sample across points.
