# Moving a previously rewarded joint sequence toward the current observed ball

The learner used 6,526 optical and encoder windows from 336 rewarded movements. It made small direct-joint changes after its ordinary search failed to find a nominal legal motion. Across 32 matched normal points it made 171 returns against the ordinary learner’s 196. The median rose from 2.5 to three and the longest point rose from 23 to 43, but thirteen points shortened and only seven improved. Six ordinary points and two treatment points reached 15. On 39 physically matched incoming balls, thirty succeeded for both arms, seven failed for both, and two ordinary successes became misses. The higher peak does not justify deploying the new controller.
