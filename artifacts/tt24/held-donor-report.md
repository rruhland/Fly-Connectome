## Source fitting and physical release

The earlier integrated-stress trial qualified a frozen bundle. The broad-start motor fit trained with immediate releases. This pass put the held phase inside each donor training rollout. Three altered hinged donors began from independently widened joint angles. A separate, seeded dispenser supplied a position, velocity and spin. Each draw independently chose a fixed-world stock dispenser or a dispenser on the corresponding donor body. Each donor also had a different fixture displacement, ball mass, upper-link inertia, drive and end-clamp camera payload. The fixed ball position varied independently of the learner's starting joints.

The simulator held the ball at that location for 12–40 control ticks while the policy moved the five ordinary actuators. The fixed constraint ended at release. The original physical rebound referee then scored the freely moving ball. A training episode could include two gravity shifts, randomized initial gravity, contact damping, drag and starting offsets. Simulated source perception had access to physical state with randomized metric position bias and causal velocity lag. The target policy received wrist RGB and own encoders.

Training began from the carried 5×20 weights. The source search changed all five joint outputs using signed local weight proposals informed by the 30 unscored donor motor probes. Its training score used physical rebounds, consecutive runs and time capped at 170 control ticks. Selection used held donor starts with a 240-tick cap. An immediate-release donor roster vetoed candidates below 87% of the original's ordinary source utility. The allocation was 2,346 stressed practice drops, 3,105 candidate-validation drops, and 45 initial validation drops. The interruption recovered the same saved weights and proposal RNG before resuming source simulation.

## Sealed target procedures

The direct learned head and frozen head used the same two carried optics calibrations, calibration identification, equilibrium reference and actuator envelope. A separate retention comparison used the original matrix whenever the same existing public image matcher recalled stock calibration and the trained matrix whenever it recalled the changed calibration. Both matrices were tested directly on each physical body. The appearance selector received pixels only and was fitted before this study.

The fresh stressed sets crossed stock and alternate installations with world-fixed and body-served dispensers. Independent release heights, shifts, spins and velocities came from the dispenser seed and a small additional fixture offset. The physical trial used the integrated-stress gravity schedule and an eight-second cap after release. Late runs were counted up to the cap. The six ordinary drops per body used an immediate release and the usual five-second cap. Optical acquisition is the carried observer's accepted sightings around the release. First and second rebounds are separately scored physical events.
