# Starting a complete earned joint movement earlier

The earlier-retrieval controller produced 94 legal returns on 16 ordinary continuously random points. Stock made 130 from the same initial motor memory and opening/draw assignments. Its best game increased from 23 to 31, but the median fell from 5.5 to 4.5. Starting earlier helped individual points and cost too many others. The initial bank contained 1,664 camera and encoder samples from 336 previously scored movements, carrying up to 43 actual future joint references instead of the usual 21. The model compared nominal and perturbed sensor-initialized trajectories from two earlier points on the incoming flight. Prospective acceptance required both perturbations to produce legal modeled returns. It installed an early movement 137 times in 216 searches and saved 486 further early samples from actual returns. The controller recorded an issued five-tick correction from two misses. No incoming game state or opponent request was available to the learner.
