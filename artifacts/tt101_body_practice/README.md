# Does extra practice on a different clamp help on a new opponent stream?

After 256 update-eligible deliveries on the shifted clamp, the learner returned to the original centered installation. Before- and after-practice motor memories played the same 24 previously unused measured releases with independent continuous opponent draws. Both memories were frozen throughout assessment. The new starts were sampled without replacement from 328 eligible recordings. All 24 points count. [Assessment roster and exclusions](assessment.json)

| Centered assessment | Total own returns | Median | Zero | Reached 5 | Reached 15 | Best |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Before extra practice | 151 | 2 | 6 | 9 | 3 | 34 |
| After shifted-clamp practice | 173 | 4.5 | 5 | 12 | 6 | 21 |

Ten points improved, five shortened and nine tied. The average paired difference was +0.92 returns per point. Its 95% release-bootstrap interval spans −3.13 to +4.63. The previous improvement from 73 to 175 over 16 different starts was larger. On this new set the direction of the median repeated and the size of the gain remains uncertain. The equal-dose control practices on the centered clamp. The strongest evidence of learning on an individual delivery came from opening 9322. Both copies touched the same low right-wing ball. Before practice the ball landed on the arm's own half. After practice it landed legally across the net and the arm made six returns. A fresh simulation of both complete action tapes reproduced the events and checked 748 and 5,393 physics substeps respectively, with positive CAD clearance throughout. Across all physically identical incoming balls, both arms succeeded on 20, both missed seven and the practiced arm alone returned this one. On 16 shared balls where both copies scored with different contacts and both next flights were measured, the average change in the projected height of the next ball was −5.8 mm. Its interval spanned −30.9 to +20.3 mm. The gain on this set cannot be attributed to routinely drawing higher replies. [Paired physical departures and next shots](replication-shot-effects.json)
