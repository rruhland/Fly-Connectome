# Wrist-only regulation rally

Moving the same 30 × 28 mm board by approximately 36 mm cleared the gripper's upcourt sightline from the lateral ready posture. The new aperture is 79 mm from the gripper frame origin. Two short standoffs support the rotated board. The direct table clamp and stock arm remain in use. The tested lens has a 102° vertical field of view and captures native 640 × 480 RGB. The motor learner carried an existing bank of rewarded stereo-based actions acquired on the same measured opening family. ## Verified continuous point

The refined controller required at least seven usable exposures. It rechecked the remaining stroke on fresh wrist evidence after the table bounce, with a 3 mm nominal clearance margin and a preference for centered blade contact. That version produced **22 alternating legal returns** on measured opening 7271, with 11 from the SO-101. Median departure speed was 5.49 m/s overall and 5.09 m/s from the near arm. The point lasted 16.4 simulated seconds. The action replay reproduced all 114 referee and contact events. Its full-CAD audit covered 7,874 physics states, including the initial and terminal states, with zero penetrations and a minimum checked clearance of 17.07 mm. Peak measured joint motion reached 6.11 rad/s, above the motor's 5.45 rad/s no-load reference. Stroke selection used physics search, and the trial took 461 wall-clock seconds on the shared machine.
