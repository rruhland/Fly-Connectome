# Learning when to accelerate a previously successful joint movement

The no-backprop readout used recent camera and encoder history to predict how far the joint commands would change three, seven or eleven control ticks ahead in paid demonstrations. The arm chose among those leads and the original response using past actual return rewards. Three of five axes showed useful held-release predictive skill across most leads. In 32 complete normal games, the modified arm made 129 legal returns versus 196 for the original, with medians 1.5 and 2.5. The best respective points reached 21 and 23. Prediction of later demonstrated movements was insufficient evidence for moving the arm sooner on new deliveries.
