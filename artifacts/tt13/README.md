# Table tennis after a change of controls

**The player can recover new motor assignments during a game on two simulated arms.** It identified four unseen signed wirings after a single ordinary point each. Across six complete games, online remapping won three and landed 90 legal returns. Keeping the earlier motor map won none and landed 36. The controls changed between points. The learner already knew how to track the ball and blade and how to attempt a stroke. [Watch the five-axis arm win 14–12 after its original controls return](five-revisit.mp4) · [Scores, controls, transfer and reproduction](live-ports.md)

| Research question | What the games showed |
| --- | --- |
| Can normal play identify a control change? | Both arms recovered their exact new and restored assignments. With the changed controls active, remapping earned 30 points and made 54 legal returns. The fixed motor maps earned zero and made zero. |
| Can that experience help a different task? | In 3D target pursuit on the same two rewired bodies, the transferred player spent 3,234/6,144 frames on target versus 2,431 starting in pursuit. It beat the target-only player on 29 of 32 paired path-and-view trials. |
| Can choosing return depth win more points? | The gantry won both games with online placement, versus one with its original stroke. On identical playable balls the frozen learned depth won 6/13 versus 7/13 for the original stroke. Transferring placement to the five-axis arm lost 6–11 versus the original stroke's 11–4. |
| Can the six-joint rotary arm sustain a rally? | Reusing earlier rotary geometry produced five legal first returns in 14 opponent-service starts across two games, versus one in 14 for an equal-probe fresh fit. Both players lost 2–11 and 4–11. Neither landed a second return on an opponent-served point. |
| Did native Pong survive? | Both the five-axis and rotary checkpoints reproduced all 180 held native Pong action tapes and scored the same 41 points. |

The motor-map learner and tactical placement learner ran as separate interventions. The rotary geometry comparison held service control, optical registration and receiving timing fixed across branches. Each lane records the simulator events separately from the visual observations and public rewards available to the player.
