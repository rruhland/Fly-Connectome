The new teacher kept the standard clinic action on strong, answerable estimates. On a weak outcome or no suitable contact, it sampled different locations along the actual incoming flight. Legal candidates were ranked for lateral margin, a more central bounce, net clearance, speed and post-bounce progress toward the far paddle. Failed impacts supplied a dense loss for the next round. Only one selected motor tape acted on the live point. No body, ball, placement scheduler, or opponent motion was reset within a point. The simulator's exact state was supplied to the teacher only. ## A cheap RGB signal for the camera-only planner

An RGB-only observer for outgoing shape is useful as an escalation signal. From recent stereo frames it detects the near-blade motion change, fits a short outward trajectory, and draws a small set of sensitivity variants for first bounce and the longest contiguous receiver-height window. The model stores only camera-derived observations. Truth is opened separately to score the diagnostic. At 30 Hz it forecast all 21 selected legal strokes with a median 135 ms wait after impact and median errors of 7.8 mm laterally and 6.7 cm longitudinally. At 60 Hz the corresponding figures were 102 ms, 7.3 mm and 7.7 cm. Processing 899 already-triangulated 30 Hz observations for these estimates took 0.30 CPU seconds, excluding image capture and triangulation. These clips are related prefixes of the same long release, not a held calibration population. [RGB tracks](motorcoach-visual-rgb.json.gz) · [Separate evaluator labels](motorcoach-visual-evaluator.json) · [Per-stroke results](motorcoach-visual-results.json)

The old unanswered 19th scored zero even under the optimistic shape variants. Both actual answered replacements scored one at the nominal 30 Hz forecast. But a hard veto on that full geometric grade would also have rejected five of the 18 genuinely answered strokes. The old high lob's bounce depth alone was off by 18 cm. A narrower, deliberately conservative rule flagged only a volley whose sensitivity variants all projected |x| above 0.55 m and y below 0.35 m. It flagged the one observed no-reply case and none of the 18 answered cases in this small sample. Two capped final balls had no reply opportunity and were not scored as misses. This is an exploratory heuristic, not a calibrated probability or independent validation. [Causal forecaster and soft grade](../../scripts/experimental/tt38_motorcoach_optics.py)

For a deployed camera-only planner, use the same light geometry on estimates from the visual twin or a learned sensory motor model, preserve the existing safe stroke when depth is ambiguous, and ask for better alternatives when the wide-short alarm is confident. The post-hit RGB score can be trained against later physically observed replies, but it cannot change a shot already issued. Do not feed the native teacher's privileged state, hidden spin, reply seed or referee event time to the learner.
