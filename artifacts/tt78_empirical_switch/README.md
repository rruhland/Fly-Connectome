# TT78 | An empirical switch cannot rescue a ball without a playable candidate

The stock camera receiver returned 11 of 32 matched training offers. None of the 37 alternative actions on the 21 stock misses landed legally. Twenty-one nevertheless made real racket contact. Twelve sent the ball backwards, and nine sent it forward without landing a legal return. A hindsight selector with exact knowledge of every fired alternative could not improve on stock.

| Exact stock outcome | Alternative actions | Actual racket contacts | Legal and CAD-clean alternatives |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 21 missed deliveries | 37 | 21 | 0 |
| 11 scored deliveries | 22 | 21 | 6 |

The six legal alternatives all received an ordinary opponent answer. Across 4,304 replayed CAD substeps on those alternatives, there were no penetrations. One legal alternative was vetoed by the nominal optical twin, but stock had already returned that ball. One other nominally legal alternative lost a stock winner. No offered incoming hit or bounce diverged across these 59 counterfactuals and their 32 ordinary controls at the repository's numerical tolerance.

## The actual decision

Two existing centered direct-joint movement families made the proposals. TT74 transports locally identified motor responses and TT59 recalls low-rank repairs from physically scored strokes. Exactly one candidate per available family was sampled uniformly from up to four distinct contact-feasibility finalists. A soft modeled separation cost ordered menu entry, but modeled legality was neither required nor used to rank the menu. Branch order was also randomized. Every selected movement visited the actual native plant from the exact same attained arm and ball as its ordinary action control. Prefixes replayed archived RGB-controlled play. Issued alternatives used standard joint reference and rate limits.

The switch targeted the matched binary difference in native legal-return reward, not the absolute probability of a candidate returning the ball. Its features came from the current RGB flight, stock camera search, trusted joint and applied reference readouts used by proposal generation, and approximate public-twin response to the candidate. The regression never used true current ball state, future action or score, release ID, TT37's sampled goal, or other referee internals as input. Legal native outcome and CAD were training labels and evaluator witnesses only.

The frozen docket used 32 distinct training releases and 18 wholly different held release IDs. Both rosters were stratified using the previous stock camera search and left/center/right optical lane, not on known stock success or intervention outcome. This is a branch-based causal test on screened contexts, not a whole-game return-rate estimate.

## Admission and limits

Leave-one-release-out predictions tied stock at 11/32 by never switching. Always taking the estimated best candidate, with no advantage threshold, fell to 6/32. Taking the best candidate only when the nominal twin rated it legal fell to 10/32. Neither selector could uncover a rescue in this menu. The 18 held motor forks and ten prospective full points were not opened.

This result narrows the failure to candidate generation for these two existing action families at this optical decision gate. It does not establish that there is no causal selector, or that the missed balls have no successful centered motion. A larger fitting model or a looser veto is not the next informative use of trials. A new action family should first produce several independent native improvements on matched stock misses, as well as worthwhile preservation controls, before testing the switch again.
