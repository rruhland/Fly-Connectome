# TT88 · Centered arm in ordinary RGB points

The body-only accuracy gain did **not** yield a new public stroke in this test. The player tried to construct motor goals from ordinary camera estimates and a bank of contact-relative endpoints from actual legal returns. The bank contributed target poses and normal speeds, never the corresponding control tapes. The exact current ball, spin, and desired contact time were not supplied. A separate predictor reconstructed from stereo and encoders had to find a legal shot before ordinary issuance.

On the two exact developmental receives, 58 fixed-lead and 58 learned trajectories were compared at identical public target windows. Neither arm produced a nominal legal trajectory or a legal offset probe. The original full event records, including the incoming far contact and near bounce, were unchanged.

The two ordinary full points were the first two in the frozen TT83 playable docket, with the original carried actor and independent opponent draws. The learned branch evaluated 30 roots on five of seven eligible windows and retained all 15 instances where the normal actor already had a robust choice. On one window in 6487 a root landed under an optical stress offset but not the nominal estimate. It did not clear the declared real-issuance gate. These complete games reproduced the exact stock action and referee tapes. They prove safe noninterference at those settings, not an ability to return the missing balls.

A reusable TT83 warm-root adapter is exposed in `PublicRoots.roots(engine, reference)`. The method reads only the synchronized public twin, measured encoder history, known issued references and pre-existing learned endpoint bank. Its result is a set of relative joint reference programs. The ordinary point runner used these roots directly under a fixed-size public gate, not as a large new population search.

## Check the one uncertainty-only candidate

A separate, precommitted one-fork diagnostic issued the 6487 option that had been legal only under an optical stress offset. On the exact physical incoming, it hit 73.5 mm from center, sent the ball down at 3.81 m/s, and lost to the floor. The ordinary control returned that same incoming. This supports retaining the nominal gate on that case. The diagnostic was not added to the player or counted as a full game. [Exact calibration fork](stress-matched.json.gz)
